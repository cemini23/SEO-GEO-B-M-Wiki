## Raw Concept

Entity page for Patreon — established subscription platform for creators, strongest for mainstream/SFW content. 10% flat platform fee (as of August 2025). Explicit AI content policies for Adult/18+ tiers. `[Source: support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/34055590411789-Understanding-Patreon-s-AI-policies-for-Adult-18-creators (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` `[Source: thepodcasting.org/patreon-new-rules-2026-ai-content-minor-safety-adult-content-policy-changes-explained (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`

## Narrative

Patreon is the longest-established subscription platform (launched 2013), built on a membership model rather than pay-per-view. It is **strict on NSFW content** in public-facing spaces but permits adult content on pages categorized as Adult/18+. AI-generated content is allowed with disclosure requirements.

### Platform mechanics `[CONFIRMED]`

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Platform fee** | 10% flat (as of August 2025; previously 5-12% tiered) `[Source: support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/36426991446797-A-standard-platform-fee-for-new-creators-effective-after-August-4-2025 (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` `[Source: ruzuku.com/learn/articles/patreon-pricing (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` |
| **Payment processing** | 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (Stripe) |
| **NSFW content** | Allowed on Adult/18+ pages; **public-facing spaces must be SFW** (profile image, banner, tier descriptions, free member posts) `[Source: support.patreon.com/policy/guidelines (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` |
| **AI content policy** | Allowed if disclosed; different rules for Safe for All vs Adult/18+ pages `[Source: support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/34055590411789-Understanding-Patreon-s-AI-policies-for-Adult-18-creators (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` |
| **iOS app fee** | Apple takes 30% (drops to 15% after 1 year); Patreon auto-adds ~43% price increase on iOS `[Source: ruzuku.com/learn/articles/patreon-pricing (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` |
| **Features** | Free trials, Autopilot, Gifting, Discounts, Tier Repricing (unlocked after migration to new fee structure) |

### AI content policy `[CONFIRMED]`

| Content type | Policy |
|--------------|---------|
| **AI-generated (non-realistic)** | Permitted on all page types if disclosed (animated, illustrated, written, audio) |
| **Hyperrealistic AI** | Permitted only on Adult/18+ categorized pages; tutorials/how-to guides prohibited on any page type |
| **AI deepfakes (non-consensual)** | Banned across all page types |
| **AI disclosure** | Required — creators must label AI-generated content appropriately |
| **Public-facing AI NSFW** | Prohibited — profile image, banner, tier descriptions must be SFW regardless of page category |

`[Source: support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/34055590411789-Understanding-Patreon-s-AI-policies-for-Adult-18-creators (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`

### Monetization structure `[CONFIRMED]`

1. **Monthly memberships** — tiered subscriptions ($1-$100+ range typical)
2. **Pay-per-post** — individual paid posts (Patreon-exclusive)
3. **Tips/donations** — one-time payments
4. **Patron-only Discord** — community access via Discord integration
5. **Merchandise** — integrated merch store (select tiers)

### 2026 policy updates `[CONFIRMED]`

Patreon rolled out comprehensive Community Guidelines updates in 2026 after months of creator consultation `[Source: thepodcasting.org/patreon-new-rules-2026-ai-content-minor-safety-adult-content-policy-changes-explained (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`:

- **Minor safety**: stricter verification and content restrictions around minors
- **AI content clarification**: clear guidelines distinguishing allowed AI art from prohibited deepfakes
- **Adult content refinement**: more structured rules for NSFW pages, clearer definitions
- **Creator collaboration**: policy shaped through active engagement with creator base (not top-down)

### When to choose Patreon `[CONFIRMED]`

| Good for | Not ideal for |
|-----------|--------------|
| SFW creators (podcasters, artists, educators, writers) | NSFW creators needing public-facing nudity/teasers |
| Long-term recurring relationships | Quick PPV monetization |
| Building community + Discord integration | Creators wanting AI chat automation (none built-in) |
| Creators wanting established brand trust | AI-first creators (Fanvue is better) |
| Mainstream audience appeal | Adult content as primary offering |

### Comparison: Patreon vs OnlyFans vs Fanvue `[CONFIRMED]`

| Factor | Patreon | OnlyFans | Fanvue |
|--------|---------|----------|---------|
| **Platform fee** | 10% flat | 20% flat | 15-20% (after intro) |
| **NSFW policy** | SFW public; NSFW on Adult/18+ only | NSFW fully allowed | NSFW fully allowed |
| **AI content** | Allowed if disclosed; hyperrealistic = Adult/18+ only | Allowed with disclosure | Explicitly supported, core use case |
| **Internal discovery** | Weak (some browse) | None | Yes, algorithm-driven |
| **Payout speed** | 5-7 business days | 5-7 business days | Immediate |
| **Best for** | Mainstream creators | NSFW traditional creators | AI content + virtual influencers |

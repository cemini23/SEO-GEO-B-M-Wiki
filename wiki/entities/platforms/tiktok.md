## Raw Concept

Entity page for TikTok — short-vertical-video platform with a discovery-heavy, interest-graph algorithm. The top discovery platform for creators in 2026 with 200B+ daily Shorts views. Primary role in creator marketing is as a **growth engine**, not a revenue engine. Direct monetization is modest; real value is audience acquisition funneled to subscription platforms.

## Algorithm Mechanics (2026)

TikTok uses a **tiered distribution model** on the For You Page:

1. **Phase 1 — Initial test** (100–300 viewers): If engagement signals are strong, advances
2. **Phase 2 — Expansion** (1K–10K viewers): Algorithm measures watch time, completion, shares
3. **Phase 3 — Broad push** (10K–100K+): Content enters broader FYP rotation
4. **Viral breakout** (100K+ views): Requires sustained engagement across all metrics

**Top 7 ranking signals (in order)**:
1. Average watch time (most weighted)
2. Completion rate
3. Replays/loops
4. Shares (DM shares weighted highest)
5. Comments (quality-weighted; emoji-only deprioritized)
6. Saves
7. Profile visits

**Key 2026 changes** `[CONFIRMED — sources/tiktok-marketing-2026.md]`:
- **Follower-first testing**: New videos shown to existing followers first, then expanded based on performance
- **70% completion rate** now needs for viral push
- **Longer content (1–3 min)** gets algorithmic boost over short clips
- **Original audio** gets discovery preference
- **TikTok SEO**: Nearly 40% of Gen Z prefers TikTok search over Google — keywords in captions, on-screen text, and voiceovers boost discoverability
- **Comment quality** over quantity: thoughtful comments weighted more than emoji reactions

## Creator Monetization (2026)

| Stream | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Creativity Program Beta** | Replaced old Creator Fund; rewards longer-form (>1 min). Requires 10K+ followers + 100K+ views in 30 days |
| **Brand deals** | Nano ($50–$250/post) → Mega ($20K–$100K+/campaign) |
| **TikTok Shop** | In-app selling; GMV Max Ads use AI-pulled UGC in paid slots (5–8% in-app conversion) |
| **Live gifting** | Real-time tip revenue during streams |
| **Affiliate marketing** | Commission-based product promotion |

**US TikTok Deal implications (2026)**: New governance, data privacy, and moderation requirements. Algorithm audits may slow feature rollouts. Recommended strategy: diversify across YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, build owned channels (email, newsletter).

## Content Strategy Data `[CONFIRMED — sources/tiktok-marketing-2026.md]`

| Element | Recommendation |

**Multi-platform stat**: Multi-platform creators earn **$65K/year median** vs. $38K for single-platform creators (ConvertKit data). TikTok = growth engine; YouTube = revenue engine.

**Shorts subscriber conversion**: 1M Shorts views ≈ 500–5,000 subscribers (vs. 20K–50K long-form). Hybrid strategy (Shorts → subscribers → long-form) grows channels **41% faster**.

## Narrative

TikTok's algorithm is more discovery-driven than Instagram's — content from accounts with zero followers can reach hundreds of thousands of viewers if the algorithm signals are right (watch-completion, rewatch, shares, comments). For a barbershop in 2026, TikTok is a high-leverage acquisition channel for younger demographics, especially when content uses trending sounds + before/after or in-progress cut clips.

Content cross-posting note: TikTok's algorithm penalizes Instagram-Reels-watermarked content. Operators should **re-edit per platform**.

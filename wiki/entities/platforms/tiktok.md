## Narrative

TikTok's algorithm is more discovery-driven than Instagram's — content from accounts with zero followers can reach hundreds of thousands of viewers if the algorithm signals are right (watch-completion, rewatch, shares, comments). For a barbershop in 2026, this makes TikTok a high-leverage acquisition channel for younger demographics, especially when content uses trending sounds + before/after / in-progress cut clips.

## Narrative

Yelp combines a business directory with a review surface and is consumed by Apple Maps + Siri + Alexa as well as direct yelp.com traffic. Yelp's recommended-vs-not-recommended filter is famously aggressive — many real positive reviews get filtered to "not recommended" by their algorithm, especially from new reviewer accounts. This is the dominant tactical pain point for operators on Yelp; it cannot be circumvented (the filter applies regardless of operator action), only worked-around by encouraging customers to write reviews from established Yelp accounts.

`[NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-05-07]` for the current state of the filter algorithm and Yelp's policy on operator-engagement with the filter.

Yelp's solicitation rules differ from Google's: Yelp explicitly forbids businesses from asking for reviews. This is stricter than Google's policy and is enforced via account warnings + filter-aggressiveness on flagged businesses.

## Raw Concept

Entity page for the Instagram platform — content surfaces, algorithm behavior, account-management mechanics. The barbershop-specific playbook for *using* Instagram is at @concepts/social-media-for-barbershops.md.

## Narrative

Instagram is a Meta-owned visual social platform with multiple distinct content surfaces: the Feed (static posts + carousels), Stories (24-hour ephemeral), Reels (short vertical video, 15-90s), and direct messaging (DMs). For a barbershop in 2026, **Reels carry the algorithmic-reach traffic**, and **the Feed carries the portfolio depth** that converts a discoverer into a follower.

### Content surfaces

| Surface | Primary purpose for barbershops | Notes |
|---------|--------------------------------|-------|
| Reels | New-customer reach + discovery | Algorithm pushes to non-followers; trending sounds matter; 15-30s sweet spot |
| Feed (static + carousel) | Portfolio depth + follower retention | Before/after carousels are highest-saving content |
| Stories | Soft updates, polls, behind-the-scenes | Ephemeral; don't try to use for portfolio |
| Story Highlights | Pinned curated Stories on profile | Use for "before/after," "team," "services," "location" |
| Profile bio + link | Conversion funnel | Bio + link-in-bio destination |
| DMs | Booking inquiries + customer service | Increasingly load-bearing; respond fast |

### Account types

- **Personal**: not for businesses
- **Creator**: hybrid; analytics + monetization tools
- **Business**: full analytics, scheduling via Meta Business Suite, ad eligibility, contact buttons (Call / Email / Directions)

For a barbershop: **Business** account, linked to the shop's Facebook Page (required for some features).

### Algorithm signals (load-bearing)

`[CONFIRMED — sourced via industry research, 2025-2026]`: The signals that historically dominate:

- **Saves** (highest-leverage signal in 2024-2026): users saving a post is the strongest "this is good content" signal
- **Shares** (especially via DM): high-leverage, especially for Reels
- **Watch time / completion rate** (Reels): higher completion = more reach
- **Comments**: weighted heavily, especially comments with multiple words
- **Profile visits** + **follows from a single post**: signals algorithm-relevance

What's *less* important than commonly believed:
- **Likes**: weighted, but lower than saves and shares
- **Hashtag count**: not the multiplier it was 2018-2020; quality > quantity

### Multi-location accounts

For a two-shop operator: Instagram does not natively support multi-location business profiles like GBP does. Options:

1. **Single brand account** + per-location detail in posts/Stories. Easier to grow; less location-specific.
2. **Per-shop accounts** (e.g. `@shopname.<neighborhood-1>`, `@shopname.<neighborhood-2>`). More authentic for each neighborhood; double the work; harder to grow.
3. **Brand + per-shop accounts simultaneously**. Most expensive in time; sometimes worthwhile for franchises.

See @concepts/social-media-for-barbershops.md for the recommendation framework.

### Cross-posting + content reuse

- **Reels → Facebook Reels**: Meta supports native cross-posting (no watermark penalty)
- **Reels → TikTok**: TikTok algorithm penalizes Instagram-watermarked content; re-edit per platform
- **Reels → YouTube Shorts**: re-export without watermark, can help cross-platform discovery
- **Feed posts → Facebook Page**: native cross-post via Meta Business Suite

### Instagram Subscriptions (2026)

Instagram now natively supports **subscription-based monetization**:
- Creators can charge **$0.99–$99.99/month** for exclusive content (subscriber-only Stories, Lives, Reels, broadcast channels)
- **100 subscribers at $2.99/month ≈ $299/month** before ~30% Apple/Google fees and taxes
- **Key risk**: Building subscriptions solely on Instagram is "rented land" — diversify by selling subscriptions off-platform (e.g., Stan Store, own website) `[Source: stanstore.com (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`

**Proven Reels-to-subscription strategies**:
- **Teaser-to-paywall**: Share first 30 seconds of a tutorial as a public Reel → prompt subscription for full content
- **Exclusive Reels**: Post 2–3 subscriber-only Reels/week
- **Survey subscribers** to understand content preferences before investing in production

**Shopping tags in Reels**: Provide a clean conversion path — one educational campaign drove **18% of a brand's website traffic** in 60 days `[Source: contesimal.ai (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`.

### 2026 AI Tools

AI-powered Reel creation tools are expected to simplify: auto-generated captions, music sync assistance, content repurposing suggestions.

The link-in-bio destination is the alternative funnel — typically a Linktree / Beacons / native multi-link page directing to: booking, GBP review form, location pages, Yelp.

### Policy + ToS

- **Branded content**: paid promotion needs disclosure
- **Before/after with minors**: requires parental consent; some US states + non-US markets have stricter rules (`[NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-05-07]` for the operator's specific jurisdiction)
- **DM auto-replies**: bots that auto-DM are forbidden; native auto-reply for "out of hours" is fine
- **Buying followers**: forbidden; Meta filters out fake follows over time

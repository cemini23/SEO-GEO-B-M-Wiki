## Raw Concept

Entity page for Fanvue — AI-powered creator monetization platform with explicit AI content support. Launched 2020 by Joel Morris, Will Monange, Harry Fitzgerald. $22M Series A (2026), $100M+ annualized run rate, 200K+ creators. `[Source: businesswire.com/news/home/20260112458975/en/ (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` `[Source: bizjournals.com/bizjournals/news/2026/02/25/fanvue-seriesa-ai-expansion-creator-monetization.html (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`

## Narrative

Fanvue is the leading platform explicitly built for **AI-generated content and virtual influencers**, while also supporting traditional creators. It positions itself as the "AI-native" alternative to OnlyFans, with built-in AI tools for content creation, fan interaction, and chat automation.

### Monetization options `[CONFIRMED]`

1. **Monthly subscriptions** — tiered pricing ($5-20 typical range) `[Source: apatero.com/blog/fanvue-complete-guide-ai-creators-monetization-2026 (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`
2. **Pay-per-view (PPV)** — individual content pieces sold separately
3. **Tips** — one-time payments from fans
4. **Custom requests** — personalized content for premium fans
5. **AI agent upsells** — automated DMs that offer paid content (human-in-the-loop required for compliance)

### GTM & Monetization Strategy `[CONFIRMED — sources/fanvue-gtm-blueprint-2026.md]`

The Fanvue GTM Blueprint outlines a four-pillar go-to-market strategy for synthetic creators:

1. **Niche Selection** — Three high-profit verticals: Personal Finance/AI SaaS (RPM $10–15), Hyper-Local B2B AI Consulting (retainers $3K–$20K), Fantasy/Historical Lore Storytelling (RPM $12.82, <10K competing channels). Niche creators achieve 3.7x profitability advantage over generalists.

2. **GEO Traffic Architecture** — Anchor persona to local SEO-GEO wikis for AI engine discovery; use semantic chunking and JSON-LD schema for AI extraction; cross-platform content mix (70% short-form video / 30% static).

3. **Conversion & Retention** — Freemium-to-PPV pricing model; OpenRouter LLM 4-layer prompt architecture (Character Bible → Fan Memory RAG → Separated Selling Logic → AI Chaperone) for parasocial DM sales. Average $40+/user in PPV revenue.

4. **Brand Partnerships** — SaaS affiliates (20–30% recurring), AI ad creatives (+12% CTR), media kit with verified benchmarks.

### Content policy highlights `[CONFIRMED]`

- **AI-generated content**: explicitly permitted and platform-encouraged
- **Disclosure**: AI content must be disclosed (labeled as AI-generated)
- **Deepfake prohibition**: non-consensual deepfakes are banned; Hive detection enforces
- **NSFW AI content**: allowed if properly categorized and disclosed
- **Character/influencer accounts**: core platform use case — virtual models, AI personas

### Growth strategy on Fanvue `[CONFIRMED]`

- **Fanvue has internal discovery** — unlike OnlyFans, has some algorithm-driven creator discovery `[Source: supercreator.app/guides/onlyfans-alternatives (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`
- **Faster growth than OnlyFans** — less saturated, algorithm favors new creators
- **Direct traffic still matters** — use Twitter/X, Reddit, Instagram for external promotion (same funnel as OnlyFans)
- **Character consistency** — AI creators need consistent visual identity across posts

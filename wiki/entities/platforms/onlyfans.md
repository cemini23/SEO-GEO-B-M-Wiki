## Raw Concept

Entity page for OnlyFans — the primary subscription content platform for creators. Covers platform mechanics, monetization models, content policies, analytics, and growth levers. The creator-marketing-specific playbook for *using* OnlyFans is at @concepts/creator-content-strategy.md and @concepts/creator-audience-growth.md.

## Narrative

OnlyFans is a subscription-based content platform where creators charge fans a monthly fee for access to exclusive content, plus optional pay-per-view (PPV) messages for premium content. For image-based creators (photographers, models, influencers), it is the dominant platform as of 2026.

### Monetization models (2026 benchmarks)

| Model | How it works | Best for | 2026 Revenue Share |
|-------|----------------|----------|---------------------|
| **Monthly subscription** | Fans pay $4.99–$49.99/month for access to all wall posts | Core recurring revenue; set based on content volume + perceived value | ~30-35% of top creator earnings |
| **PPV (pay-per-view)** | Premium content sent via DM for a one-time fee ($5–$200+) | High-value exclusive content; limited-time offers; custom content | ~59%+ of top creator earnings (overtook subscriptions in 2023) |
| **Tips** | Fans can tip on posts or via DM ($1-$100) | Bonus revenue; often tied to engagement milestones | ~5-10% of earnings |
| **Custom content** | Personalized photos/videos at $50-$500+/minute | Highest per-hour revenue; limited scale | Varies by creator |
| **Live streaming** | Real-time interaction with tipping | Engagement + tip revenue; builds parasocial relationships | Small % of total |
| **Referral program** | Earn 5% of referee earnings | Passive income for established creators | Supplemental |

**Key 2026 finding**: After OnlyFans' 20% fee + payment processing (2.9% + $0.30), creators net approximately 75-78% of gross subscriber payments. `[CONFIRMED - multiple agency sources]`

**PPV dominance**: By 2023, ~59% of top creator earnings came from PPV/content sales, overtaking subscriptions as primary revenue stream. This trend deepened in 2026. `[CONFIRMED - thewebaddicted.com, B9 Agency]`

### Subscription tiers strategy (2026 data)

Most successful creators use **3–5 tiers** with clear value differentiation:

- **Entry tier ($9.99–$14.99)**: High-volume, low-barrier. Access to main feed only. Goal: convert free followers. `[CONFIRMED - B9 Agency, onlysonar.com]`
- **Mid tier ($24.99–$39.99)**: Added perks — priority DM replies, occasional PPV discounts, behind-the-scenes content.
- **VIP tier ($49.99–$99.99+)**: Custom content quota, 1-on-1 video calls, exclusive Telegram/Discord access, all PPV included.

**2026 pricing benchmarks**:
| Tier | Price Range | % of Creators | Strategy |
|------|-------------|------------------|----------|
| Entry | $4.99-$9.99 | Majority | High-volume conversion |
| Sweet spot | $9.99-$19.99 | Most successful | Balance volume + revenue |
| Premium | $20-$49.99 | ~20% | Niche positioning |
| Platform average | $7.21 | All creators | Heavily skewed by low-end |
| Platform cap | $49.99 | Maximum allowed | Cannot exceed |

**High-ticket insight (2026)**: Subscriptions priced $15-$25 with lower volume but higher retention are outperforming low-ticket models by 40%. `[Source: SirenCY 2026 creator data]`

**PPV ladder strategy (2026)**:
| Tier | Price | Content Type |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Tier 1 (low) | $5-$10 | Photo sets, quick teasers, mini clips |
| Tier 2 (mid) | $15-$50 | Premium clips, exclusive content |
| Tier 3 (high) | $50-$200+ | Custom content, personalized requests |
| Customs | $50-$500+/min | Negotiated rates based on creator status |

### Content surfaces (2026 data)

| Surface | Purpose | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| **Wall (feed)** | Core subscription content; visible to all paid subscribers | 1–3 posts/day typical; mix of SFW teaser + NSFW main content |
| **DMs (direct messages)** | PPV sales, custom content negotiation, fan relationships | Response time is a retention lever; creators who answer DMs in <1 hour have ~30% higher retention `[CONFIRMED - gitnux.org 2026]` |
| **Stories** | 24-hour ephemeral content; behind-the-scenes, polls, casual updates | Lower production value OK; higher frequency builds parasocial connection |
| **Live stream** | Real-time interaction with tipping | Builds loyalty; converts free viewers to subscribers |
| **Profile/bio** | Conversion hook for profile visitors (from external traffic) | Clear value prop + link to Twitter/X or Linktree |

**DM engagement stats (2026)**:
- Response time <1 hour: ~30% higher retention `[CONFIRMED]`
- PPV open rates: ~35% average `[Source: gitnux.org]`
- Weekend traffic: 2x weekday traffic `[Source: gitnux.org]`

### Algorithm and discovery (2026 verified)

OnlyFans has limited native discovery compared to social platforms `[CONFIRMED - multiple sources]`:

- **No algorithmic feed**: subscribers see content chronologically
- **No hashtag search**: discovery happens almost entirely OFF-platform (Twitter/X, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram)
- **Recommended creators**: shown to subscribers `[TENTATIVE]` — the recommendation algorithm is proprietary and not publicly documented; reverse-engineering suggests it surfaces creators sharing audience overlap with the subscriber's existing subscriptions. Not externally influenceable.
- **Internal search**: weak; don't rely on it for growth
- **Referral program**: 5% of referee earnings for verified creators

**Key implication**: External promotion (see @concepts/creator-external-promotion.md) is the primary growth lever, not platform-native discovery. X (Twitter) dominates referrals `[CONFIRMED - DataGlobeHub 2026]`.

**2026 traffic source hierarchy**:
1. **X (Twitter)** — primary referral source, dominates social media referrals
2. **Reddit** — high-converting but high ban risk
3. **TikTok** — discovery platform, short-video content
4. **Instagram** — visual platform, Reels for promotion

### Content policies (2026 updates — critical)

**2026 Major Policy Overhaul** `[CONFIRMED - list25.com, sirency.com]`:
- **AI-generated content**: Must be clearly labeled as artificial; platform uses AI/ML tools for automated review
- **Deepfake ban (2026)**: AI-generated explicit content depicting real individuals (deepfakes, face-swaps) = **immediate permanent ban**
- **Enhanced verification**: Stricter identity confirmation requirements rolled out in 2026
- **Geographic compliance**: Alignment with EU Digital Services Act (DSA) and UK Online Safety Bill

**Core platform policies**:
- **NSFW allowed**: OnlyFans permits adult content (unlike Instagram/TikTok) — this is its core differentiator
- **No sexual intercourse on live stream**: certain acts prohibited even on paid platforms
- **ID verification required**: government ID + selfie; payout delay until verified. Processing typically 24–72 hours per B9 Agency `[TENTATIVE]` — published times vary by country and ID type.
- **DMCA protection**: creators can report stolen content; platform takes down infringing copies `[CONFIRMED - onlyfans.com/terms]`
- **Chargeback risk**: high-chargeback categories face higher reserve requirements
- **Terms of Service**: Creators grant OnlyFans rights to submit DMCA notifications on their behalf, but OnlyFans is under no obligation to police infringements `[Source: onlyfans.com/terms]`

**Enforcement** `[TENTATIVE]`: Specific enforcement patterns, prohibited-content list comprehensiveness, and appeals process timelines are not publicly documented by OnlyFans. Operator-facing baseline: assume DMCA + content-policy enforcement is automated-first with manual review for appeals; budget 7-30 days for appeals.

### Funnel Optimization (2026) `[CONFIRMED — sources/onlyfans-funnel-optimization-2026.md]`

**Average conversion rates**:
| Creator Level | Conversion Rate |
|---------------|----------------|
| Average creators | <2% |
| Optimized funnels | 8–15%+ |
| Top creators | 15%+ |

**Traffic source rankings by conversion rate**:
1. **Reddit** — 3–8% conversion (highest intent, highest ban risk)
2. **Twitter/X** — 2–5% conversion (direct link clicking)
3. **Instagram** — 1–3% conversion (visual discovery)
4. **TikTok** — 0.5–2% conversion (younger, lower-intent audience)

**Two-page funnel strategy**: Free page → Paid page conversion significantly outperforms direct-to-paid. The free page reduces subscriber risk and improves long-term revenue.

**Welcome sequence**: Critical first 24 hours — personalized welcome, immediate value delivery, first PPV within days 6–7.

**Value ladder**: Subscription ($4.99–$49.99) → Tip menu ($5–$50) → Custom content ($100–$300) → Premium experiences ($200–$500).

**Key retention insight**: Keeping subscribers for 6+ months is more profitable than constant new signups. High-ticket subscriptions ($15–$25) with lower volume but higher retention outperform low-ticket models by 40%.

### Payout structure (2026)

- **Platform fee**: 20% of all revenue (creator keeps 80%) `[CONFIRMED - multiple sources]`
- **Payout threshold**: minimum $20–$50 depending on method
- **Payment methods (fans)**: Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Maestro — all require 3D Secure verification `[Source: B9 Agency]`
- **Payout timeline**: 3–5 business days after 7-day pending period `[CONFIRMED - pleazeme.com]`
- **Payout frequency**: weekly or bi-weekly; **weekly recommended** to limit exposure (account suspensions freeze pending balances) `[Source: B9 Agency]`
- **Chargeback reserves**: platform holds reserve against disputed payments; new creators face higher reserves

**Net calculation**: After OnlyFans' 20% fee + payment processing (2.9% + $0.30), creators net approximately 75-78% of gross payments `[Source: onlysonar.com, B9 Agency]`.

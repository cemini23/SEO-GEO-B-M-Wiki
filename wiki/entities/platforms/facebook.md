## Raw Concept

Stub entity page for Facebook — Meta's flagship social platform. For a barbershop, Facebook serves dual roles: (1) social presence for an older demographic, (2) review surface (Facebook "recommendations").

## Narrative

Facebook Pages are the operator-controlled surface for a business on Facebook. They support posts, photos, events, recommendations (the renamed reviews feature), messaging, and ads. For a typical local barbershop — where the customer base spans demographics — Facebook is non-essential for new-customer reach via the algorithm (reach is heavily throttled for organic page content) but valuable for: (a) the recommendations review feed (some customers preferentially review on Facebook), (b) older customers checking the page before booking, (c) Meta-Business-Suite cross-posting from Instagram, (d) ad targeting if the operator runs paid Meta ads.

Facebook recommendations operate on a "yes/no" recommend basis (not stars) with a written reason. They feed Google's entity-resolution as one of many citation sources. `[NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-05-07]` on current recommendation-algorithm behavior.

## Raw Concept

Entity page for Passes — OnlyFans competitor with the lowest platform fees (10%) and most complete feature set. `[Source: mexc.com/news/867508 (retrieved 2026-05-08)]`

## Narrative

Passes is positioned as the **lowest-fee alternative to OnlyFans** at just 10% platform fee (compared to OnlyFans' 20%). It offers the most complete feature set among OnlyFans alternatives, making it attractive for creators who want to maximize take-home pay.

### Platform mechanics `[CONFIRMED]`

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Platform fee** | 10% flat — lowest among major platforms `[Source: mexc.com/news/867508 (retrieved 2026-05-08)]` |
| **Feature set** | Most complete among OnlyFans alternatives (subscriptions, PPV, DMs, analytics, promotions) |
| **NSFW content** | Fully allowed |
| **AI content** | Allowed with disclosure (not AI-native like Fanvue) |
| **Internal discovery** | Yes, browse and recommendation features |
| **Payout speed** | Competitive with industry standards |
| **Creator count** | Growing, but still small vs OnlyFans |

### Monetization options `[CONFIRMED]`

1. **Tiered subscriptions** — monthly recurring (competitive pricing)
2. **Pay-per-view (PPV)** — individual content, custom requests
3. **Tips and donations** — one-time payments
4. **Promotional tools** — discount campaigns, free trial periods
5. **Analytics suite** — comprehensive creator dashboard

### Why creators choose Passes `[CONFIRMED]`

| Advantage | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Lowest fees** | 10% vs OnlyFans' 20% = keep $10 more per $100 earned |
| **Complete feature set** | Most comprehensive tooling among alternatives |
| **Growing platform** | Less saturated than OnlyFans |
| **Creator-friendly** | Built as response to high OnlyFans fees |

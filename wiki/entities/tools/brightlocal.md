## Raw Concept

Stub entity page for BrightLocal — a local-SEO-focused suite covering citation tracking, NAP-audit, review management, rank tracking, GBP audit, and white-label reporting.

## Narrative

Where Semrush and Ahrefs are general SEO suites, BrightLocal is purpose-built for local SEO. Its load-bearing features for a barbershop operator: (a) **citation tracker** — finds existing listings of the business across the directory ecosystem and flags NAP inconsistencies, (b) **review monitoring** across GBP/Yelp/Facebook in one feed, (c) grid-based local-pack rank tracking, (d) GBP audit (completeness check).

The "find citations" + "fix NAP inconsistencies" workflow is the strongest fit for a typical local SMB. For a brand-new operator getting set up: BrightLocal's citation-find tool quickly surfaces the existing data Google has on the business — old listings that may be outdated, duplicates from Yelp/Yellow Pages aggregators, etc. — and these are then corrected manually.

Phase-0 audit caveats: BrightLocal **does not** offer review gating tools (their public stance is anti-gating, which is good). Some of their automated-citation-submission features have historically been criticized; manual / supervised submission is preferred over auto-submit.

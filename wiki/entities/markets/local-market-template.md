### Local cultural / language context

Many markets have cultural patterns that materially shape marketing decisions:

- **Bilingual or multilingual customer base**: signage / IG content / review-language strategy may need to address languages other than English
- **Diaspora populations**: portfolio depth showing cross-cultural / cross-demographic capability matters in some markets
- **University adjacency**: brings annual cycle of new students — recurring acquisition opportunity each fall semester
- **Industry / employer concentration**: e.g. military base, hospital, factory, university — drives lunch-hour and end-of-shift demand patterns
- **Heritage / town identity**: historic theming, cowboy / coastal / industrial / etc. branding can be a differentiation lever

### Local citation directories worth pursuing

Generic top-tier directories (GBP, Yelp, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yellow Pages, Foursquare) plus market-specific directories that may exist in the operator's geo:

- **Local Chamber of Commerce listings** (city or county chambers — typically a paid membership; evaluate against ROI)
- **Local newspaper / city-guide directories** (the major local newspaper site usually has a business directory)
- **Niche / industry directories**: industry-specific listing sites for the operator's category (Booksy / Vagaro / StyleSeat for beauty + barbering, OpenTable for restaurants, etc.)
- **Neighborhood-focused directories** (Nextdoor, Patch, etc.)

This list becomes a citation-building checklist — see @concepts/citation-building.md once populated. Pursuing directory listings without confirming reputation can backfire: spam directories give negative signal. Apply the Phase-0 audit lens (see @concepts/citation-building.md) before submitting to any unfamiliar directory.

### Multi-location geographic strategy

If the operator has multiple shops in the same market, geographic positioning is the primary determinant of:

- **Service-area overlap** (cannibalization risk if shops are <5 miles apart on the same arterial road)
- **Per-shop website-page targeting** (e.g. shop 1 page targets [neighborhood A] + adjacent suburb; shop 2 page targets [neighborhood B] + a different adjacent suburb)
- **Per-shop GBP content differentiation** (each must have unique photos, distinct services if they differ, distinct hours)

The actual coordinates + neighborhoods of each individual shop belong on the per-shop entity pages (see @entities/companies/shop-1.md / shop-2.md). This page provides the surrounding-market context.

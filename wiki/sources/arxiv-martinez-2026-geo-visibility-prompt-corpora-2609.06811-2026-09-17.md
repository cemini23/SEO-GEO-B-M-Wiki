## Narrative

Follow-on measurement treatise from the same author as @sources/arxiv-martinez-2026-critical-survey-geo-2607.14035-2026-07-16.md (K140). **No new experiments** — critical framework for what GEO visibility scores actually measure.

**Answer market:** prompt corpus + weights define an evaluative "answer market" that may not match real user demand. Prompt wording shifts retrieval, competing sources, and generated answers. LLM-based scoring instructions can change scores on unchanged answers.

**Framework components:** situation annotation, prompt formulations, execution conditions, weights, scoring rules. When weights are unknown, report **sets of admissible scores** (partial identification vs normative sensitivity). Distinguishes documentary leave-one-out comparison from full-engine intervention with competing sources.

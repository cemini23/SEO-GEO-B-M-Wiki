## Narrative

TikTok in 2026 remains the highest-reach discovery platform for creators, but the algorithm and monetization landscape has shifted significantly from earlier years.

### Algorithm Mechanics (2026)

TikTok uses a **tiered distribution model** (For You Page testing):

1. **Phase 1 — Initial test**: Video shown to 100–300 viewers. If engagement signals are strong, advances.
2. **Phase 2 — Expansion**: 1K–10K viewers. Algorithm measures watch time, completion, shares.
3. **Phase 3 — Broad push**: 10K–100K+ viewers. Content enters broader FYP rotation.
4. **Viral breakout**: 100K+ views. Requires sustained engagement across all metrics.

**Top 7 ranking signals (in order)**:
1. Average watch time (most weighted)
2. Completion rate
3. Replays/loops
4. Shares (DM shares weighted highest)
5. Comments (quality-weighted, not emoji-only)
6. Saves
7. Profile visits

**Key 2026 changes**:
- **Follower-first testing**: New videos shown to existing followers first, then expanded based on performance
- **70% completion rate** now needed for viral push
- **Longer content favored**: 1–3 minute videos get algorithmic boost over short clips
- **Original audio boost**: Using original sounds gets discovery preference
- **TikTok SEO**: Nearly 40% of Gen Z prefers TikTok search over Google — keywords in captions, on-screen text, and voiceovers all boost discoverability
- **Comment quality over quantity**: Emoji-only reactions deprioritized; thoughtful comments weighted more

### Creator Monetization (2026)

| Stream | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Creativity Program Beta** | Replaced old Creator Fund; rewards longer-form content (>1 min). Requires 10K+ followers and 100K+ views in 30 days |
| **Brand deals** | Nano ($50–$250/post), Micro ($250–$1K+), Mid ($1K–$5K+), Macro ($5K–$20K+), Mega ($20K–$100K+) |
| **TikTok Shop** | In-app selling; GMV Max Ads use AI to pull affiliate UGC into paid slots (5–8% in-app conversion) |
| **Live gifting** | Real-time tip revenue during streams |
| **Affiliate marketing** | Commission-based product promotion |
| **Subscriptions** | Growing feature for recurring creator revenue |

### Content Strategy Data

**Optimal posting cadence**: 3–5 posts/week; consistent posting grows accounts 67% faster with 89% higher retention.

**Video length guide**:
- 15–30 sec: trends, quick tips
- 30–60 sec: sweet spot for most content
- 60 sec–3 min: deeper storytelling (gets Creativity Program boost)
- 3–10 min: established audiences only

**Best-performing formats**: Story-driven content, before/after transformations, explainer/searchable content, reaction/commentary, list-style content, day-in-my-life.

**Hook strategy**: Start with payoff, question, or most visually striking moment. Avoid slow intros, greetings, title cards.

**Posting times**: 7–9 AM, 12–1 PM, 6–9 PM (target audience timezone).

### Multi-Platform Imperative

Multi-platform creators earn a median of **$65K/year** vs. $38K for single-platform creators (ConvertKit data). TikTok serves as the **growth engine** — not the revenue engine.

### US TikTok Deal Implications (2026)

New governance, data privacy, and moderation requirements. Algorithm audits may slow feature rollouts. Recommended strategy: diversify across YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, build owned channels (email, newsletter).

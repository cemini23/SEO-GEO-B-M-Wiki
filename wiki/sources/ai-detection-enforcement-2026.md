## Narrative

The AI detection and enforcement landscape for creators has shifted dramatically in 2025–2026, driven by deepfake proliferation, federal legislation, and platform-level crackdowns. Creators using AI assistance must understand the boundaries between "AI-assisted" (permitted) and "AI-generated" (prohibited or restricted).

### OnlyFans AI Policy (2026) — Current

**Three-tier AI content framework**:

| Category | Status | Requirements |
|----------|--------|--------------|
| **AI-enhanced editing** (filters, lighting, skin smoothing) | ✅ Allowed | Must be of your own verified likeness |
| **AI-manipulated content** (face swaps, deepfakes of real people) | ❌ Banned | Immediate permanent ban; no warning |
| **Fully AI-generated personas** (no real human) | ❌ Banned | Persona must match ID-verified account holder |

**Required disclosure**: All AI-manipulated content must carry `#AI` or `#AIGenerated` tags in captions.

**Detection capability**:
- **95–98% accuracy** at upload using AI analysis + human moderation + user reporting
- **Biometric matching** introduced in 2026 — goes beyond basic ID checks
- Automated detection tools: **Hive Moderation**, **Reality Defender**
- Content videos face **stricter scrutiny** than images (movement, lighting, skin texture analysis)

**Enforcement actions (2026)**:
- **35,865 accounts removed** in February 2026 alone for policy violations
- **12,000+ accounts** banned for deepfake violations (2024–2025 period)
- Penalties: account closure, forfeited pending earnings, possible law enforcement referral
- **Appeal window**: 72 hours for content removal; 6 months for account termination disputes

### Federal Law: TAKE IT DOWN Act (Signed May 19, 2025)

**Key provisions**:
- Publishing nonconsensual intimate imagery (including AI deepfakes) is now a **federal crime**
- **Platform compliance deadline**: May 19, 2026
- Platforms must remove flagged content within **48 hours**
- Penalties: **2 years in prison** (adults), **3 years** (minors), plus forfeiture of proceeds and mandatory restitution
- Platforms must implement **perceptual hashing / fingerprinting** for duplicate detection
- **Critical nuance**: "Consent to creation ≠ consent to publication" — essential for collaborative creators

**Criticism**: EFF has flagged concerns about lack of counter-notification process and risk of weaponized takedowns.

### Creator Compliance Checklist (2026)

- [ ] All AI-manipulated content labeled with `#AI` or `#AIGenerated`
- [ ] No deepfakes or face-swaps of any real individuals
- [ ] Verified account holder matches the person in all content
- [ ] AI chatbots not used to impersonate the creator in DMs
- [ ] Understanding of "consent to creation ≠ consent to publication" for collaborative work
- [ ] Regular audits of content library for compliance with updated platform policies
- [ ] Awareness of federal TAKE IT DOWN Act requirements

## Dead Ends

- Assuming AI detection tools are perfect — they have significant false positive rates, especially for non-native English content.
- Relying on platform-specific detection as sole compliance strategy — federal law (TAKE IT DOWN Act) adds criminal liability independent of platform enforcement.
- Using fully AI-generated personas on OnlyFans or Fansly — explicitly banned on both platforms.

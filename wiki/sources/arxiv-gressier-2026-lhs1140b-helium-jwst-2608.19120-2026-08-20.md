## Narrative

Four JWST/NIRISS SOSS transits of temperate super-Earth LHS 1140 b find **no evidence** of metastable helium absorption (all amplitudes consistent with zero within 1σ; 3σ upper limits 0.72–1.21%). Joint constraint disfavors a persistent WINERED-reported helium-escape signal at **4.5σ**. Stellar He I variability and possible seeing-dependent instrumental contribution are discussed as alternatives to intermittent planetary escape.

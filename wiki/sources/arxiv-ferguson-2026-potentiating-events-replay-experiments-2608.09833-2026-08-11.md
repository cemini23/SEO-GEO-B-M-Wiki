## Narrative

Introduces **analytical replay experiments** to evolutionary computing: restart a population's evolution from different historical time points to sample the distribution of what could have evolved, quantifying how a population's potential for a given outcome changed with its history. Provides a step-by-step design guide plus a demonstrative genetic-programming example showing potentiation for problem-solving success does not necessarily track fitness.

## Narrative

Tool-Lab study of **LLM surrogate shoppers** — how agentic systems apply **human pricing and choice heuristics** when acting as proxy consumers. Primary domain is **marketing / agentic commerce audit**, not cyber-primary. Use for client work when you evaluate **agentic checkout**, comparison shopping agents, or **GEO/offer presentation** (fees, discounts, framing) that might steer an automated buyer.

Pairs with ConsumerQ-style audits: ConsumerQ scores **what chatbots recommend**; surrogate-shopper work stresses **decision rules under price cues** when the model executes a shopping task.

## Narrative

Google’s June 2026 messaging: **“Good SEO is good GEO.”** AI Mode and AI Overviews sit on the **same index + quality systems** as classic Search — not a parallel bot-first optimization stack.

**Operator rules from summary:**

| Google says do | Google says skip (for Google Search) |
|----------------|--------------------------------------|
| Useful, non-commodity content with first-hand expertise | Bot-first copy, artificial fragments, inauthentic mentions |
| Technical crawl/index clarity, structured data, page experience | **llms.txt** and other speculative AI-only files |
| Strong GBP + product feeds for local/commerce facts | Separate “GEO department” with untested gimmicks |
| GSC generative AI performance reports + business outcomes | Third-party AI visibility scores as sole truth |

**Mechanics cited:** RAG grounding in indexed pages + **query fan-out** (parallel related searches) — brands must earn eligibility across supporting evidence, not only the head query.

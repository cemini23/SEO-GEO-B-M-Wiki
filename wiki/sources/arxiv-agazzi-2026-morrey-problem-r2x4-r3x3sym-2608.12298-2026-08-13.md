## Narrative

Constructs an explicit rank-one convex but non-quasiconvex integrand in ℝ^{2×4} (falsifying the quasiconvexity inequality via a map T⁴→ℝ² with 12 non-zero Fourier modes, obtained from a scalar potential — also yielding a rank-one convex non-quasiconvex integrand in ℝ^{4×4}_sym), via transpositions and restrictions of Grabovsky's ℝ^{8×2} example; also modifies Šverák's example to build a rank-one convex non-quasiconvex integrand in ℝ^{3×3}_sym. Advances the long-open Morrey problem (whether rank-one convexity implies quasiconvexity), which is essentially equivalent to lower semicontinuity/coercivity of the associated functional.

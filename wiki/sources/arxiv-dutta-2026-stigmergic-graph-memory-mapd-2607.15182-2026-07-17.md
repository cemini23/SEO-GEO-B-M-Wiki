## Narrative

**SGM** = bounded decaying memory on warehouse graph nodes/edges that ranks **which** feasible pickup/delivery endpoints enter the MAPF planner (not only how agents route after goals are fixed). Paired benchmarks: **+20.5–36.7%** throughput vs reconstructed M2M / M2M-wSKU baselines across 15 map–load conditions.

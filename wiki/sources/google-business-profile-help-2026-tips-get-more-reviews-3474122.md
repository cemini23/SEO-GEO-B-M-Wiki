## Narrative

Google's **official** review acquisition + reply guidance for GBP `[CONFIRMED]`:

### Acquisition (allowed)

- **Remind customers** via Google review link or **QR code** (GBP dashboard tools).
- **Reply to reviews** — shows feedback matters.
- **Value all reviews** — honest mix of positive/negative builds trust; reply to share perspective.
- Reviewers need a **Google Account** (non-Gmail email OK).

### Prohibited (explicit on page)

- **Incentives** (free/discounted goods/services) in exchange for reviews, review edits, or negative review removal = **fake & misleading content**, strictly prohibited.

### Reply best practices

- Professional, polite, short, **conversational not promotional** (reviewers are already customers — no deals in replies).
- Vary replies — avoid identical “thank you” to everyone.
- **Negative reviews:** flag policy violations; don't share reviewer private info; apologize when appropriate; personalize; respond timely; take complex issues offline.

> "Offering incentives, like free or discounted goods or services, in exchange for customers to post reviews, change reviews, or remove negative reviews is considered fake & misleading content and is strictly prohibited."

> "A mix of positive and negative feedback often feels more trustworthy."

> "Be conversational, not promotional: Your reviewers are already customers, so avoid using your response to offer deals or promotions."

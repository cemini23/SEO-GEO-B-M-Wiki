## Narrative

Classification of complex generalised weighing matrices CGW(n,w;k) that admit a primitive monomial automorphism group of small rank/degree, via centraliser algebras of Schur covers. Recovers known geometric families, adds Hamming-scheme families, settles small open existence cases, and constructs quantum error-correcting codes with exact minimum distances from the matrices.

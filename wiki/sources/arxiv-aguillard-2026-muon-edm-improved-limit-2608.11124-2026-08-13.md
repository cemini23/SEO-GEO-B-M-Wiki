## Narrative

New direct limit on the permanent electric dipole moment (EDM) of the positive muon from Fermilab Muon g-2 data (2019–2020 run). Tracking detectors measure the average vertical decay angle of positrons from muon decays, probing an interaction between a possible muon EDM d_μ and the lab-frame magnetic field. Result: d_μ = (−0.35 ± 0.19_stat ± 0.34_sys) × 10⁻¹⁹ e·cm — consistent with zero — setting |d_μ| < 1.10 × 10⁻¹⁹ e·cm at 95% CL. EDMs violate parity and time-reversal; a nonzero value would signal CP violation beyond the Standard Model (relevant to the matter–antimatter asymmetry).

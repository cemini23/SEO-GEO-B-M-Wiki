## Narrative

Two July 3 GBP review incidents matter for operators:

1. **Missing reviews / review blocks:** Search Engine Roundtable reports many GBP owners seeing reviews vanish, sometimes after fake-review/spam attacks. Google spokesperson quote: when systems detect suspicious reviews, Google may remove reviews or temporarily pause reviews; Google is investigating and will restore incorrectly removed reviews.
2. **Additive restrictions:** A second report shows review/rating restrictions stacking after repeated incentivized-review violations. Example notice: customers could not post or see reviews for **2 months** and saw a warning about suspicious reviews removed.

### Operator Translation

- Do not assume every review drop is a policy violation; July 2026 has a reported bug/investigation path.
- Capture screenshots/export counts weekly so review loss can be documented.
- If fake-review attacks precede review disappearance, record dates, examples, and forum/support case IDs.
- If a restriction notice cites incentivized reviews, stop all review campaigns immediately and audit vendors against @briefs/2026-07-01_k135-review-compliance-vendor-audit-hands-on.md.
- Repeated violations can lengthen restriction windows; first-party rule remains no incentives for reviews, edits, or negative-review removal.

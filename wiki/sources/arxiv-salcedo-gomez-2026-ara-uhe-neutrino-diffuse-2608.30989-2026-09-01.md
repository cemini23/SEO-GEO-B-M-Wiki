## Narrative

**Askaryan Radio Array (ARA)** is an ultrahigh-energy neutrino detector at the South Pole. It searches for impulsive broadband radio signals from neutrino-induced showers in glacial ice. Five autonomous stations with antennas up to 200 m deep have accumulated the largest in-ice radio livetime. This paper presents the first array-wide diffuse UHE neutrino search using January 2013–December 2023 data.

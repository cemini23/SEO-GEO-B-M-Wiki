## Narrative

ML (transformers / MLPs) to establish Seiberg dualities via quiver mutations ("learning to unknot"). For ~10-node quivers, learned nets beat deterministic search; adding pathfinder algorithms ("Google Maps for quivers") further improves efficiency/accuracy. Proposed as AI-for-physics benchmark.

## Narrative

**Planetary Prediction Engine (PPE)** is an autonomous AI system (Google Earth AI) that executes end-to-end geospatial modeling from natural-language queries: synthesizes multimodal datasets on the fly from Data Commons and Google Earth Engine, fuses them with geospatial foundation model embeddings (PDFM, AlphaEarth), and searches task-tailored model architecture families with automated overfitting guards. Reported gains include US spatial regression (CDC health indicators R² 76.8% vs 60.0% baseline), FEMA risk indices, SVI, Nigerian food-security downscaling (R² 66.1% vs 31.5%), and 2026 DRC Ebola nowcasting Recall@10 83.3% (+10.3 pp vs public SOTA).

**SEO remit:** planetary-scale geospatial ML false positive — **not** local-pack / GBP / near-me GEO. Federation: **CCC thin** — autonomous multimodal data discovery + model search from NL (pairs agentic data-selection patterns). Google proprietary → no clone.

## Narrative

Bradford–Ersoy–Schneider–Thom classify the infinite simple locally finite groups that admit a mixed identity (a nontrivial word with constants that vanishes under every substitution of its variables). Apart from an explicit list of alternating, finitary linear classical, and non-simply-laced Lie-type groups, no such group exists; they also derive lower bounds on mixed-identity length in finite simple groups of Lie type and show that simple compact Lie groups admit no mixed identities.

## Narrative

Case study of GenAI as a research tool in statistics: ChatGPT-5 + ScholarAI (Sept–Nov 2025 release) used to (1) identify relevant sources for a literature review, (2) summarize papers, (3) identify research gaps, and (4) generate R code implementing methodology from academic papers — applied to dynamic treatment regime (DTR) estimation via the dynamic weighted ordinary least squares (dWOLS) approach. Evaluates the quality of the AI-produced literature review and code against expert ground truth.

## Narrative

Maps product-simplex constrained optimization to a smooth manifold via Hadamard-style reparameterization; second-order KKT on the manifold maps to weak second-order KKT on the simplex. Riemannian GD outperforms projected GD on probabilistic tensor decomposition and SRVF curve registration.

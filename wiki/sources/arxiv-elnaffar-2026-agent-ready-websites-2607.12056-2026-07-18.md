## Narrative

Positions websites as **runtime environments for AI agents**, not only human visitors. Proposes a three-axis readiness framework:

1. **Interpretability** — machine-readable structure (semantics, labels, schema)
2. **Executability** — agents can complete tasks (booking, forms, CTAs) without brittle DOM guessing
3. **Decision reliability** — accurate, temporally valid facts (hours, price, availability)

Controlled POC: agent-ready site **89.3% PASS** vs conventional **49.3% PASS** on task completion. `[TENTATIVE]` for production local B&M — lab POC, not multi-shop field study.

### Operator translation

Service sites should expose clear booking paths, labeled form fields, stable NAP/hours in HTML + schema, and avoid “pretty but empty” hero-only pages that agents cannot act on. Complements GEO citation work: if an agent cannot execute “book a fade Friday 3pm”, citation alone does not convert.

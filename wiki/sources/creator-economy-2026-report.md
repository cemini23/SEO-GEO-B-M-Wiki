- **Instagram**: Visual platform, Reels for promotion

### Key Findings

1. **Power-law economics**: The top 1% earn 33% of total platform revenue. The average of $131/month is misleading because it's skewed by ultra-high earners.

2. **PPV dominance**: By 2023, 59% of top creator earnings came from PPV/content sales rather than subscriptions. This trend has only deepened in 2026.

3. **High churn**: 50% of creators burn out within the first year. Monthly income swings affect 60% of creators.

4. **Pricing matters**: High-ticket subscriptions ($15-25) with lower volume but higher retention are outperforming low-ticket models by 40% in 2026.

5. **External traffic is everything**: OnlyFans has limited native discovery. X (Twitter) is the dominant referral source. Success depends on external promotion skills, not platform algorithms.

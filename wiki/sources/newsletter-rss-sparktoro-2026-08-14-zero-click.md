## Narrative

SparkToro’s weekly whiteboard: scholars split on the zero-click era; SparkToro still treats the **website as the only permanent home** for content — the place to own the brand experience and to inform search, AI tools, and the occasional visitor. Fewer visits does not mean the site vanishes; it remains the load-bearing asset for GEO/AEO because answer engines cite a canonical, structured owned surface.

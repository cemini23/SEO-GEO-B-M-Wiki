## Narrative

Iterative ancilla-assisted process tomography (“quantum telephone”) for multinode fiber quantum networks: previously probed links become ancillae for later links. Sequential estimation compounds errors under noisy intermediates; global inference plus downstream near-unitary channels can resolve earlier degeneracies.

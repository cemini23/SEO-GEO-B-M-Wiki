## Narrative

PsychoAgent separates factual vs affective memory streams and gates retrieval through a conflict-aware executive controller: affective traces are semantically prefiltered then re-ranked by salience. On three controlled conflict scenarios, conflict-critical recall 0.933 vs 0.500 (semantic-affective) and 0.667 (single-memory RAG), with a small semantic-similarity cost. Blinded rater overall mean favored the full architecture after within-rater standardization (+0.22 SD) but corrected pairwise diffs were not significant. Psychoanalytic labels are secondary analogies; primary constructs are dual-process control + conflict monitoring.

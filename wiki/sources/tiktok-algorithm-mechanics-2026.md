## Narrative

### How TikTok's Algorithm Works (2026)

TikTok uses an **interest graph, not a social graph** — it recommends content based on what users will enjoy, not who they follow.

#### Tiered Distribution Model

Every video goes through a multi-stage testing funnel:

| Phase | Audience Size | Gate Criteria |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| **Phase 1** | ~100–500 followers (or random sample) | Watch time percentage in first 30 seconds |
| **Phase 2** | Broader non-follower audience | Shares, saves, comments, completion rate |
| **Phase 3** | Full FYP (For You Page) | Cumulative engagement velocity |

A major 2025/2026 change: **videos are now tested with followers first before reaching non-followers**. If followers don't engage, the video dies before reaching wider audiences.

#### Completion Rate: The #1 Signal

- The completion-rate bar for viral distribution is **~70%** (up from ~50% in 2024)
- Average watch time percentage is the single most important ranking factor
- If the majority of your test audience watches 80%+ of the video, TikTok pushes it wider
- If most viewers swipe away in the first 2 seconds, the experiment is over

#### Engagement Velocity

Rapid accumulation of likes, comments, and shares in the **first hour** determines whether content gets wider distribution. Each new engagement signal tells the algorithm "this is still resonating."

#### Key Ranking Factors (2026 Order of Importance)

1. **Watch time % / completion rate** (~40-50% of algorithm weight)
2. **Shares** (now weighted above likes since 2025 update)
3. **Saves/favorites** (signals lasting value)
4. **Comments** (especially substantive, not just "nice 🔥")
5. **Replay/rewatch behavior**
6. **Original audio usage** (gets discovery boost)
7. **Content relevance** to categorized interest buckets
8. **Creator consistency** (posting 3-5x/week maintains algorithmic favor)

### The "3-Second Hook" Imperative

TikTok's algorithm evaluates whether viewers stop scrolling in the first frame. Effective hooks:

- **Visual movement** in frame 1 (gesture, zoom, scene change)
- **On-screen text** with a bold claim or question
- **Verbal hook** — a provocative first sentence delivered in the first second
- **Curiosity gap** — hint at something without revealing it

The September 2025 algorithm update introduced **advanced AI detection** for originality. Engagement bait (e.g., "wait for it..." with nothing) is penalized. The system scans editing patterns, unique audio, and user-made visuals to identify genuinely original work.

### Content That TikTok Pushes in 2026

| Format | Why It Works | Optimal Length |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| **Delayed reveal** | Forces viewers to stay for payoff | 15-45 sec |
| **Tutorial/how-to** | High save rate = algorithm boost | 30-60 sec |
| **Controversy/debate** | Drives comments and shares | 15-30 sec |
| **Relatable story** | Emotional connection → shares | 30-90 sec |
| **Unexpected comparison** | Curiosity + completion | 20-60 sec |

### TikTok SEO (2026)

- 40% of Gen Z now uses TikTok as a search engine
- Keywords in the first 3 seconds of on-screen text carry the highest SEO weight
- Spoken audio is scanned for search ranking
- Properly categorized videos (via keywords, hashtags, sounds) get shown to relevant audiences with significantly higher engagement

### What Changed in 2025-2026

- November 2025: Users gained control over how much AI content they see
- September 2025: Algorithm update favoring originality over engagement bait
- 2025 update: Shares and saves weighted above likes
- 2025 update: Completion rate bar raised to ~70% for viral distribution
- Creator Fund replaced by Creativity Program Beta (higher payouts, longer content)
- Longer content (1-3 min) now favored over pure short-form

### What Doesn't Work Anymore

- Posting low-effort content daily (signals to algorithm your content isn't worth distributing)
- Engagement bait without substance
- Watermarked cross-posts from other platforms
- Copying trending formats without adding original value
- Buying followers (destroys engagement ratio, detectable)

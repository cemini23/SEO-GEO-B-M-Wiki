## Narrative

Vanilla scotogenic model (VSM): three right-handed neutrinos + inert scalar doublet; explains neutrino masses, dark matter, and baryon asymmetry via leptogenesis. Typical scale ~10 TeV+; enhanced second-generation Yukawa can lower leptogenesis scale to ~1 TeV — probeable at future muon colliders.

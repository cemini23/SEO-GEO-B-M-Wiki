## Narrative

**pyEDITH** is a Python exposure-time calculator for the recommended NASA flagship **Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO)**: simulates wavelength-dependent exposure times and S/N for direct-imaging observations with realistic engineering specs and user-defined targets. Modular architecture for evolving mission requirements; validated against existing calculators; released open-source with API docs, tutorial notebooks, and a GUI.

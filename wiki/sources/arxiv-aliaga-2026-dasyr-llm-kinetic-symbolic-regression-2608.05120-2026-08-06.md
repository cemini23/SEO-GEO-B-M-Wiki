## Narrative

LLM module inside iterative symbolic regression (ADoK-S backbone): critiques candidate rate laws for physicochemical plausibility and proposes new structures. Cuts iterations to ground-truth by 41.7–79.3% vs SR-only on four in-silico catalysis/bioprocess cases; R² > 0.98 on validation.

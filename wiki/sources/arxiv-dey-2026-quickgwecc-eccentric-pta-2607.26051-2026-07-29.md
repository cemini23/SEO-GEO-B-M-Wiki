## Narrative

Bayesian CGW search for eccentric SMBHBs in PTA data. Splits parameters into **projection** vs **shape**; many cheap projection updates per fixed shape set (orders of magnitude faster). Validated on simulated PTA datasets.

**SEO remit:** astro-ph / GW — overflow. Thin OSINT steal: hierarchical Bayesian search with cheap vs expensive parameter blocks. Atto / TipDrop / poker / prod: no hook.

## Narrative

Discrete movable-antenna (MA) positioning for MU-MIMO uplink mutual-information maximization. Proves monotone submodular maximization under a 2-system constraint; distance-constrained submodular search ≥1/3 optimum, ≥90% of optimal MI in sims, ~34× faster than branch-and-bound; robust under imperfect CSI.

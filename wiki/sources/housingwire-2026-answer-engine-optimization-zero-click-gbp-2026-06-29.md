## Narrative

Practitioner framing of **AEO/GEO** for local service brands (real estate case study; generalizes to barbershops):

- **Zero-click rise:** ~68% of U.S. Google searches ended without a click (early 2026, SparkToro/Similarweb); **83%** when AI Overview present; **93%** in Google AI Mode (per article).
- **Mechanism shift:** SEO optimizes for **clicks**; AEO optimizes for **being cited** inside generated answers.
- **GBP as AI infrastructure:** Gemini grounded in Maps/GBP (“Grounding with Google Maps,” 250M+ places); AI Overviews use GBP for local recommendations; ChatGPT/Bing stack pulls Bing Places + directories + websites — consistency with GBP anchor matters.
- **Data governance > copy tricks:** complete categories, service areas, Q&A seeds, cross-platform NAP — accuracy drives citation frequency.

## Narrative

### Account Setup and Verification

OnlyFans requires ID verification before creators can withdraw earnings. The verification process includes government-issued photo ID + selfie matching. Verification status unlocks: payment eligibility, chat functionality, promo tools, subscriber analytics, and referral program access (5% of referee earnings). Top verification status reportedly provides 100% payout rates according to some agency sources, though this contradicts the standard 80/20 split and requires verification `[NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-05-08]`.

Verification delays can occur for new creators. Support can clarify missing documents and reset verification if needed.

### Monetization Models

OnlyFans operates on an **80/20 revenue split** — creators keep 80% of all earnings (subscriptions, tips, PPV, custom content). This is more creator-friendly than Patreon (45% for creators after payment processing), YouTube (45%), or Twitch (50% for top creators).

**Payment methods accepted from fans**: Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Maestro — all must support 3D Secure verification.

### Payout Structure

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Platform fee | 20% of all earnings |
| Creator share | 80% |
| Payout threshold | $20-$50 depending on method |
| Payout methods | Bank transfer, e-wallets (PayPal, Paxum, Cosmica — varies by country) |
| Processing time | 3-5 business days after 7-day pending period |
| Payout frequency | Weekly or bi-weekly (weekly recommended to limit exposure) |

**Important**: Account suspensions can freeze pending balances. Setting up weekly automatic payouts is recommended to limit exposure to a single week's earnings rather than a month's.

### PPV (Pay-Per-View) Content

PPV content is sent via DM for a one-time fee. By 2023, roughly 59% of top creator earnings came from PPV and one-time content sales, overtaking subscriptions as the primary revenue stream — a trend that deepened in 2026.

| PPV Type | Price Range | Use Case |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Entry teaser | $5-$10 | Photo sets, quick teasers, mini clips |
| Mid-tier | $15-$50 | Premium clips, exclusive content |
| High-ticket | $50-$200+ | Custom content, personalized requests |

After OnlyFans' 20% fee, creators net approximately 75-78% of PPV price (factoring payment processing fees of 2.9% + $0.30).

### Custom Content and Tips

- **Custom content**: $50-$200/minute for newer creators; $200-$500+/minute for top earners
- **Tips**: $1-$100 per tip on posts or via DM
- **Live streaming**: Real-time interaction with tipping enabled
- **Video calls**: Available to VIP/elite creators

### Content Policies (2026 Updates)

The platform underwent major policy overhauls in 2026, emphasizing safety, transparency, and legal compliance:

1. **AI-generated content**: Must be clearly labeled as artificial. Fully AI-generated explicit content depicting real individuals (deepfakes, face-swaps) results in **immediate permanent ban**.
2. **Enhanced verification**: Stricter identity confirmation requirements rolled out.
3. **Geographic compliance**: Alignment with EU Digital Services Act (DSA) and UK Online Safety Bill.
4. **Automated review**: Platform uses AI/ML tools to detect policy violations — not just human moderators.
5. **Terms of Service**: Creators grant OnlyFans rights to submit DMCA notifications on their behalf, but OnlyFans is under no obligation to police infringements.

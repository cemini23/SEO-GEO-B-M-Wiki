## Narrative

For a connected smooth complex projective surface X, the paper proves an effective-divisor version of the Arcara–Miles conjecture and its strict analogue: for every divisorial Bridgeland stability condition, failure of stability (or semistability) of a line bundle or its relevant shift is detected by a natural subobject associated with a non-zero effective Cartier divisor C with C² < 0. The proof combines minimal-rank destabilizers, slope Harder–Narasimhan filtrations, the Bogomolov–Gieseker inequality, and an ordered Lorentzian partial-sum estimate forcing minimal rank one.

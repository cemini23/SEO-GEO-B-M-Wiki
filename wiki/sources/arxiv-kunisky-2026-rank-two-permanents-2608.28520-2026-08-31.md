## Narrative

Bang (1976) proved per²(A) ≥ (2−2ⁿ)per(A⊗J₂) for non-negative n×n matrices. This paper sharpens the constant to 1/binom(2n,n) for **rank-at-most-two** real matrices (possibly negative entries), then derives new inequalities for finite free additive (⊞ₙ) and multiplicative (⊠ₙ) convolutions of real-rooted polynomials.

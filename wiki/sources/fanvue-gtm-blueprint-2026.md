## Narrative

This document provides an architecturally complete GTM strategy for launching a 100% AI-generated creator on the Fanvue platform. It was the foundational research for the wiki's synthetic creator vertical.

### Four-pillar structure

1. **Niche Selection & Competitive Analysis** — Three high-profit verticals identified: Personal Finance/AI SaaS (RPM $10–15), Hyper-Local B2B AI Consulting (retainers $3K–$20K), Fantasy/Historical Lore Storytelling (RPM $12.82, CPM $20–25, <10K competing channels)

3. **Conversion & Retention Architecture** — Freemium-to-PPV pricing model, OpenRouter LLM integration for parasocial DM sales, 4-layer prompt architecture (Character Bible, Fan Memory RAG, Separated Selling Logic, AI Chaperone)
4. **Brand Partnerships & Alternative Revenue** — SaaS affiliate marketing (20–30% recurring), AI-generated ad creatives (12% higher CTR), media kit verification with performance benchmarks

### "Imperfect by Design" aesthetic doctrine

The document argues that the pursuit of photorealistic AI perfection is now counterproductive. Audiences exhibit "severe digital burnout" toward hyper-realistic, flawlessly rendered AI avatars. The prescribed remedy:

- Systematic post-processing degradation: heavy film grain, authentic light leaks, subtle motion blur, naturalistic uneven skin textures
- Asymmetrical formatting, candid mid-action captures, unpolished moody lighting
- Abandonment of sci-fi/cyberpunk/futuristic aesthetics that immediately signal artificiality
- Goal: force audience to connect with **narrative and utility** rather than scrutinizing pixel-perfect artificiality

### OpenRouter LLM conversion architecture (Pillar 3)

Four-layer prompt system for high-volume personalized DM interactions:

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| 1. Character Bible | Exhaustive persona constraints — colloquialisms, emoji frequencies, negative prompts |

| 3. Separated Selling Logic | PPV catalogue as environmental context, NOT baked into persona instructions |

## Dead Ends

- The document is heavily oriented toward a **digital talent agency** operating model (multiple synthetic personas managed at scale). For a single individual creator (friend-1), the B2B consulting pillar and multi-persona management aspects are less directly applicable.
- The Mac Studio hardware dependency and local batch-generation workflow assume significant upfront capital investment ($5K+ for hardware alone).
- The "Hyper-Local B2B AI Consulting" niche (Pillar 2, Section 2.1) leverages the agency's existing SEO-GEO local wiki framework — this is not directly applicable to friend-1's personal creator brand.
- Some monetization figures (e.g., $3K–$20K B2B retainers, $200+ PPV sessions) represent top-end projections that assume significant audience maturity.

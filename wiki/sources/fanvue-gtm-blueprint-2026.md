### "Imperfect by Design" aesthetic doctrine

The document argues that the pursuit of photorealistic AI perfection is now counterproductive. Audiences exhibit "severe digital burnout" toward hyper-realistic, flawlessly rendered AI avatars. The prescribed remedy:

- Systematic post-processing degradation: heavy film grain, authentic light leaks, subtle motion blur, naturalistic uneven skin textures
- Asymmetrical formatting, candid mid-action captures, unpolished moody lighting
- Abandonment of sci-fi/cyberpunk/futuristic aesthetics that immediately signal artificiality
- Goal: force audience to connect with **narrative and utility** rather than scrutinizing pixel-perfect artificiality

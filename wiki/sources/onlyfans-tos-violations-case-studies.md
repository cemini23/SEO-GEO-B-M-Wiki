## Narrative

### Major Policy Updates (2026)

OnlyFans implemented major policy overhauls in 2026, driven by global regulatory pressures and platform safety commitments. The most impactful changes:

- **Deepfakes and AI-generated content ban**: Effective 2026, deepfakes, face-swaps, and any AI-generated explicit content featuring real individuals result in immediate and permanent ban. This followed 12,000+ accounts banned between 2024–2025 for deepfake violations.

- **Enhanced verification**: Government-issued photo ID, live selfie with liveness detection (blinking, head movement), photo holding ID with current date, periodic re-verification. Third-party verification services like Yoti process this data.

- **Geo-compliance tightened**: Content must comply with the creator's specific jurisdiction laws, not just platform general rules.

### Common Violation Patterns

**Immediate Permanent Ban (No Warning):**

1. **Deepfakes / non-consensual AI content** — Any content depicting real people created without consent. Triggers immediate permanent ban and potential legal referral to authorities.
2. **Underage content indicators** — 2257 compliance violations. The 2257 rules exist to prevent minors from appearing in adult content.
3. **Synthetic personas** — AI-generated models where no real human exists behind the account. The person in content must match the ID-verified account holder.
4. **Extreme "challenge" content** — Publicized stunts that violate Acceptable Use Policy. Case: Bonnie Blue permanently banned after OF cited extreme challenge content and a planned "petting zoo" stunt.

**Content Removal + Appeal Window:**

5. **AI-generated content without disclosure** — OnlyFans now requires creators to clearly label all AI-generated or manipulated content with `#AI` or `#AIGenerated` tags in captions. Failure leads to account suspension.
6. **Copyright infringement** — Using others' copyrighted material without permission.
7. **In-person meet arrangements** — Discussing or arranging physical meetings via platform messaging can trigger bans.
8. **Chargebacks** — OnlyFans chargebacks come directly out of earned balance, not the platform's. Serial chargebackers are tracked and banned.

### Account Removal Statistics

- **12,000+ accounts** banned between 2024–2025 for deepfake violations
- **35,865 accounts** removed in February 2026 alone for policy violations

### DMCA Takedown Process

**OnlyFans' Own Process:**
- OnlyFans has a **DMCA Takedown Policy** describing procedures for responding to reports that infringing content was published on the platform.
- The platform maintains a team dedicated to issuing DMCA takedown notices to outside websites on behalf of creators.
- Creators can also contact offending websites directly and, if necessary, take violators to court for damages.

**Third-Party DMCA Services:**
- **CopyrightShark** — Scans 12,800+ sites daily using AI detection, facial recognition, and manual verification. Starts at $63/month (or $59/month billed annually) for unlimited takedowns. Most Google removals completed within 24–72 hours.
- **OnlyGuard.io** — Combines AI detection with human review. Offers unlimited DMCA takedowns, AI-driven leak detection with facial recognition, dark web monitoring, Reddit and Telegram content removal, Google delisting.
- **Rulta** — Agency-focused DMCA and leak removal service.

**DMCA Process Steps:**
1. Identify unauthorized content (leak sites, image searches, Reddit, Telegram)
2. File DMCA takedown notice (must meet 17 U.S.C. requirements)
3. Submit to hosting platforms, Google search, file hosts, forums, social media
4. Monitor status: submitted → successful removal → escalation for non-responsive platforms
5. Check whether a leak site actually honors DMCA requests before filing (500+ site database available via CopyrightShark)

### Appeal Process

**For Content Removals:**
- **72-hour window** to appeal content removals
- OnlyFans guarantees a response within **seven business days**
- Appeal via deactivation appeal form or email support@onlyfans.com
- Subject line must contain issue and username
- Include reference number if previously received
- Provide documentation (ID, verification documents, context about content creation process)

**For Account Bans:**
- Must dispute within **six months** of notification (waiving right to dispute if missed)
- File via Appeals Policy process
- Include comprehensive documentation and maintain professional communication
- Account remains suspended during appeal process
- Delayed appeals may face additional scrutiny or rejection

**For Earnings Forfeiture:**
- Dispute via Complaints Policy
- Provide proof that 2257 compliance was done correctly
- Agencies report that most "I got banned and don't know why" situations stem from simple verification or compliance errors

- Inconsistent policy enforcement results in sudden account removals
- Income inequality: majority of creators earn below $180/month while top earners capture disproportionate revenue
- Founder Leonid Radvinsky died in March 2026; Architect Capital in talks to buy platform for $3.5–5.5 billion

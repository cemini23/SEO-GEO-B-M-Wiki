## Narrative

Nambu-Goto cosmic string loops develop cusps that emit gravitational wave bursts with a characteristic strain spectrum. Standard cusp predictions assume no backreaction. This paper reformulates the Nambu-Goto equations in a convenient gauge and studies how **gravitational backreaction** modifies loop evolution and GW emission — relevant for interpreting next-generation GW searches but wholly outside SEO/GEO.

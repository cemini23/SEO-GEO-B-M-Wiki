## Narrative

Sequential choice of the overall rank-1 item when rejected offers are erased from memory (no running record). Heavy-traffic form via planar Poisson process; optimal rule collapses to threshold strategies equivalent to imperfect-observation models. Compared throughout to classic full-information Gilbert–Mosteller best-choice.

**SEO remit:** math.PR — overflow only. Poker/gambling steal: memoryless threshold policies when the agent cannot retain relative-rank history (spot decisions under severe state compression).

**Phase-0:** OUT-OF-SCOPE. No code to adopt.

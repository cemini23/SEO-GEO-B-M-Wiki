## Narrative

Search Engine Land analysis of a **2023 Google patent** describing LLM-based **entity characterization**: collect website + public data, interpret (not verbatim extract), build hierarchical attribute graphs, and synthesize holistic entity summaries (identity, services, reputation, relationships).

**Operator steal (directional, not confirmed implementation):**

- Webpages become **evidence** for entity models — service pages, team bios, case studies, reviews, press, maps listings all feed understanding.
- Third-party inputs explicitly cited in patent narrative: **maps data, job listings, business information**, reviews/sentiment.
- Visibility in AI Overviews / Ask Maps may depend on **entity understanding quality**, not only keyword-matched documents.

Patent filings are **not** proof of live ranking systems `[TENTATIVE]` — treat as Google problem framing aligned with Knowledge Graph + E-E-A-T trajectory.

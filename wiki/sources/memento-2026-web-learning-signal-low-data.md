## Narrative

**MEMENTO** treats the **open web as a learning signal**, not a stateless retrieval API. Most low-data approaches still optimize on labeled or synthetic text; MEMENTO mimics expert practitioners who improve via repeated self-directed web exploration.

**Architecture:**

| Layer | Mechanism |
|-------|-----------|
| Within session | **Adaptive Exploration Tree (AET)** — decomposes tasks into evolving sub-questions; reflects on intermediate findings |
| Across sessions | **Dual-channel memory** — **declarative** (facts) vs **procedural** (search strategies) |

No additional model training required; gains come from accumulated interaction trajectories.

**Eval domains:** sales automation (+25.6% vs ReAct baseline) and legal research (+36.5% vs ReAct). `[TENTATIVE]` — academic benchmarks; not validated on local-SEO operator workflows.

**Operator relevance [TENTATIVE]:**

- Local SEO / GEO operators face **low-data verticals** (one shop, one city) — wiki ingest + digest loops are the human analog of procedural memory (which queries work, which sources duped).
- **Sales-automation** eval is adjacent to @concepts/high-ticket-smb-lead-generation.md outbound research — not a validated playbook swap.
- Reinforces GEO guidance: engines reward **consistent factual surfaces** built from iterative research, not one-shot AI page dumps.

## Narrative

### Platform Detection Tools (2026)

OnlyFans rolled out AI content detection tools in late 2025 and expanded enforcement in 2026. The platform uses third-party detection services to flag suspicious content:

- **Hive Moderation** — Used by OnlyFans for high-volume content scanning API. Delivers 95% accuracy for AI-generated image detection, performs well on adult content variations, maintains high accuracy across many poses and scenarios. Supports continuous moderation with reliable deepfake detection.
- **Reality Defender** — Focuses on deepfake detection, reaches 90% accuracy on hyper-realistic AI photos. Used by OnlyFans for real-time detection and flagging suspicious content.
- **CloudSEK** — Ranked best overall deepfake detection tool in 2026, balancing accuracy, intelligence, and real-time monitoring across video, audio, and images.

### Biometric Verification (2026 Enforcement)

In 2026, OnlyFans rolled out biometric matching that goes beyond basic ID checks. The verification process now includes:
- Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID)
- Live selfie with liveness detection (blinking, head movement)
- Photo holding ID with current date handwritten on paper
- Verification that the person in content matches the ID holder
- Annual re-verification to ensure identities remain current

### Detection Triggers

Content that triggers manual review or automated flagging:
- Synthetic personas with no real human behind them (immediate detection via biometric mismatch)
- Deepfakes, face-swaps, AI-generated explicit content featuring real individuals
- Content where the face does not match the verified account holder
- Hyper-realistic AI-generated images that lack natural imperfections
- "Uncanny valley" effects that suggest synthetic generation

### AI-Assisted vs AI-Generated Policy (2026)

OnlyFans allows AI-generated content when creators disclose it properly and follow Terms of Service. However:
- Fully AI-generated models (synthetic personas) violate ToS — the person in content must match the person who submitted ID
- AI-assisted content (AI editing, AI captions, AI-enhanced photos) will likely need disclosure labels by late 2026
- The EU is leading the disclosure push, but OnlyFans is applying it globally
- Required tags: `#AI` or `#AIGenerated` in captions for AI-manipulated content

### Industry Context

- Industry estimates suggest 15–20% of new OnlyFans accounts use some AI-generated content in 2026
- Adoption rates run higher among agencies and top earners, who rely on AI to scale
- Platform enforcement reduced fully synthetic accounts, yet AI-assisted content continues to grow among verified human creators
- 12,000+ accounts were banned between 2024 and 2025 for deepfake violations
- 35,865 accounts were removed in February 2026 alone for policy violations

### Detection Tool Accuracy (General)

For text-based AI detection (relevant for captions, DMs, bios):
- **GPTZero, Originality.ai, Turnitin** — Rely on perplexity and burstiness scores
- **2026 study found 61.3% average false positive rate** on essays written by non-native English speakers
- False positive rates range from 1.6% to 12% across leading tools
- Copyleaks: Enterprise-focused with LMS integrations
- ZeroGPT: 12.0% false positive rate — useful for quick checks but not reliable as sole verification

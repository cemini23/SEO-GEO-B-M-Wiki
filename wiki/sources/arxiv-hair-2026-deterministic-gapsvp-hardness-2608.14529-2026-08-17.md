## Narrative

Hair & Sahai give a **deterministic** polynomial-time reduction from 3SAT to M^ε-GapSVP_p for every constant 2 < p < ∞ and every constant 0 < ε < min{(p−2)/4p, 1/8}, and for p = ∞ the same for every constant 0 < ε < 1/8. It builds on the polynomial-gap CVP construction of OpenAI [Ope26] and the direct p > 2 SVP reduction of Hair–Sahai [HS26b]. The intro frames the result against the classical worst-case-reduction story behind lattice-based post-quantum cryptography (Ajtai, Regev, Peikert).

## Narrative

Unifies linear cryptanalysis, differential cryptanalysis, and side-channel leakage analysis in one capability-adaptive candidate-space reduction architecture, followed by reduced-space quantum verification via amplitude amplification. Formal model for candidate-space construction, adaptive filtering, verification-space reduction, and complexity characterization, with theory linking candidate-space contraction to quantum verification effort; a Hamiltonian formulation grounds the reduced-space verification physically. On statistically generated cryptanalytic observations the framework contracts a 4096-candidate key-hypothesis space to 13 candidates (≈99.683% reduction); the Grover verification requirement drops from 50 to 2 iterations.

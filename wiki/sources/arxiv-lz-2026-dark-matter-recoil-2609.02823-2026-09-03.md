## Narrative

Search for dark matter interacting with xenon nuclei in **2.84 tonne-years** with LUX-ZEPLIN. Extended nuclear recoil window to ~270 keV enables effective-field-theory and inelastic models where high-energy recoils dominate. One event consistent with **248±23 (stat)±23 (sys) keV** nuclear recoil where background expectation is low. Profile likelihood: **2.6σ** global significance (look-elsewhere corrected); **3.4σ** local max across models tested.

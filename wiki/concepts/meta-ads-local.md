## Narrative

Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ads let you place haircut and grooming offers directly in front of local residents—bypassing the algorithm to put your "Book Now" button in front of people who've never heard of you.

### Campaign Objectives

| Objective | When to Use | Expected Result |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Traffic | Driving bookings to website/booking page | $0.50-2.00 per click |
| Conversions | Tracking actual bookings (requires Meta Pixel) | $15-30 per new client |
| Brand Awareness | New shop launch, grand opening | High reach, low immediate conversion |
| Lead Generation | Collecting phone numbers for SMS follow-up | $3-8 per lead |

### Targeting Strategy

**Geographic**: 3-5 mile radius from shop (tight radius = higher conversion). Run separate campaigns for each shop location.

**Demographic**: 
- Men 18-65 (core audience)
- Parents (back-to-school season)
- Grooms-to-be (wedding season targeting)

**Interest-based**: 
- "Men's grooming", "beard care", "haircuts"
- Competitor followers (target people who follow rival barbershops)
- Lookalike audiences based on your best existing clients

### Creative Best Practices

**Visual-first**: barbershops sell transformations. Use:
- Before/after haircut photos (highest engagement)
- Short Reels/clips of fades being cut (15-30 seconds)
- Shop interior + team photos (builds trust)

**Copy formula**: [Hook] + [Offer] + [CTA]
- "First time with us? Skin fade + beard trim for $25 — Book now"
- "Back to school season. Fresh cut for $20. Appointments filling fast"

**Video vs Image**: video ads get 20% higher CTR for barbershops. Even simple phone-recorded clips of a fade in progress outperform static images.

### Instagram vs Facebook Placement

| Placement | Best For | Notes |
|------------|----------|-------|
| Instagram Reels | Younger clients (18-35) | Highest engagement for barbershops |
| Instagram Feed | Brand building | Requires high-quality visuals |
| Facebook Feed | Broader age range (30-55) | Better for promos + events |
| Stories | Time-sensitive offers | 24-hour shelf life; create urgency |

### Retargeting

Most people who click "Book" don't complete. Retarget them:
1. **Pixel-based**: anyone who visited your booking page in last 30 days
2. **Engagement**: people who watched 50%+ of your video
3. **Instagram profile visitors**: people who checked you out but didn't book

Show different creative: "Still thinking about that fresh cut? We have Tuesday slots open."

### Budget Guidelines

| Level | Daily Budget | Strategy |
|-------|--------------|----------|
| Test | $5/day | One service, one radius |
| Growth | $10-20/day | 2-3 campaigns, A/B test creatives |
| Scale | $30+/day | Full funnel: awareness + conversion + retargeting |

### Local Page vs Central Brand

For multi-location shops: **each location runs ads from its own Facebook/Instagram page**. Local pages perform measurably better than national accounts with radius targeting — ads feel like community recommendations rather than corporate marketing [Source: Marvia 2026].

### Key Metrics

- **CTR**: 1-3% is healthy for image ads, 2-5% for video
- **CPC (Cost Per Click)**: $0.50-2.00 for local targeting
- **Conversion rate**: 3-8% from click to booking
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**: Aim for 4:1 minimum (spend $100, generate $400 in bookings)

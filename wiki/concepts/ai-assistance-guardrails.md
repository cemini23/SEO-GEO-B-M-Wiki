## Raw Concept

This page was created because a friend is a new OnlyFans creator who wants to use AI assistance (specifically Claude) for content creation tasks. The goal: maximize the benefits of AI assistance while staying within platform policies, avoiding detection-related issues, and maintaining authentic creator voice. OnlyFans' 2026 policy updates made this a critical topic — deepfakes and undisclosed AI-generated content now trigger immediate permanent bans.

## Narrative

### What NOT To Do

**1. Don't ask AI to write full posts verbatim**
- Platforms use detection tools (Hive Moderation, Reality Defender) that flag AI-generated content
- OnlyFans requires `#AI` or `#AIGenerated` tags for AI-manipulated content — failure to disclose leads to account suspension [CONFIRMED]
- Text detectors (GPTZero, Originality.ai) have up to 61.3% false positive rates on non-native English writing, but platforms use proprietary tools with higher accuracy [TENTATIVE]

**2. Don't use AI for NSFW content generation**
- OnlyFans prohibits deepfakes, face-swaps, and AI-generated explicit content featuring real individuals — immediate permanent ban [CONFIRMED]
- A synthetic persona with no real human behind it violates ToS — the person in content must match the ID-verified account holder [CONFIRMED]
- 12,000+ accounts were banned between 2024–2025 for deepfake violations [Source: list25.com]

**3. Don't automate DMs**
- Automated messaging triggers ban risk — DMs drive 60–70% of creator income, but AI chatbots can't match human emotional connection
- Mass automated outreach violates platform policies across OnlyFans, Twitter/X, and Instagram

**4. Don't copy-paste AI captions without editing**
- Identical content across posts triggers duplicate-content flags
- AI detection tools look for "predictable, formulaic" patterns with low perplexity and burstiness scores
- OnlyFans rolled out biometric matching in 2026 that goes beyond basic ID checks

### What TO Do

**1. Use AI for brainstorming angles**
- Generate content ideas, themed post series, seasonal campaigns
- Claude excels at structured brainstorming — give it your niche, audience demographics, and goals
- Take the ideas and adapt them to your voice and specific audience

**2. Use AI for editing/refining YOUR content**
- Write your draft first, then ask AI to suggest improvements for clarity, flow, grammar
- Ask for multiple caption variations based on your draft
- Request tone adjustments ("make this sound more casual", "add humor", "soften the tone")

**3. Use AI for caption variations**
- Generate 5–10 variations of your own caption to test which resonates
- Keep the core message but experiment with hooks, CTAs, emoji usage
- Always personalize the final choice — add specific details from your day, your personality

**4. Use AI for PPV pricing strategy**
- Ask Claude: "I have X subscribers, my niche is Y, what's a good pricing tier for PPV content?"
- Provide your actual numbers and goals — AI can analyze pricing patterns
- Cross-check recommendations with your own subscriber feedback and sales data

**5. Use AI for research/summaries**
- Research platform policy updates (like the 2026 OnlyFans changes)
- Summarize competitors' public strategies (from their free posts, tweets)
- Research trending hashtags, content formats, seasonal opportunities

### The Human-in-the-Loop Rule

**Every AI output must be:**
1. **Personalized** — Add your own experiences, feelings, specific details
2. **Edited** — Rewrite at least 30–50% of AI-generated text in your voice
3. **Infused with creator's voice** — Your slang, your humor, your pacing, your opinions
4. **Fact-checked** — Verify any claims, dates, platform policy details

**The "Claude起草, You Finalize" workflow:**
```
1. You: Draft rough ideas/bullet points/outline
2. Claude: Expand, suggest, refine, give options
3. You: Rewrite in your voice, add personal details, cut what doesn't fit
4. You: Final edit for tone, add specific calls-to-action
5. You: Post with your authentic voice
```

### Claude-Specific Workflow

**Research Summaries:**
- Ask Claude to research platform updates (e.g., "summarize OnlyFans 2026 policy changes")
- Request source citations so you can verify before acting
- Use research to inform your content strategy, not replace your judgment

**Content Calendar Planning:**
- Provide: your niche, posting frequency, special dates/events, promotional goals
- Claude outputs: weekly themes, content mix suggestions, reminder checklist
- You adapt: swap in your real life events, adjust for your energy levels, personalize themes

**Caption Brainstorming:**
- Provide: the photo/video context, your mood, what you want to convey, 2–3 hashtags
- Claude outputs: 5–10 caption options with different angles
- You rewrite: combine the best parts, add your voice, include specific details

**PPV Pricing Analysis:**
- Provide: subscriber count, current pricing, content type, niche benchmarks
- Claude outputs: pricing tier suggestions, bundle strategies, promotion timing
- You decide: based on your actual subscriber feedback and sales data

**What Claude is NOT for:**
- Generating NSFW images or descriptions (policy violation)
- Writing full posts without your input (detection risk)
- Automating DMs or comments (ban risk)
- Replacing your authentic voice (audience can tell)

### Key 2026 Platform Facts [CONFIRMED]

- OnlyFans allows AI-assisted content (editing, captions, enhancements) with proper disclosure
- AI-generated models (synthetic personas) are banned — full stop
- Required disclosure tags: `#AI` or `#AIGenerated` in captions for AI-manipulated content
- 35,865 accounts were removed in February 2026 alone for policy violations
- Appeal window: 72 hours for content removal, 6 months for account termination disputes
- The platform uses Hive Moderation and Reality Defender for AI detection [Source: b9-agency.com, 2026]

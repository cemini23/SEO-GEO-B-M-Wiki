## Raw Concept

Hub for source stubs that were ingested but, on triage, were judged **not aligned with this wiki's domain** (local SEO, GBP optimization, GEO/AEO, web design, social/creator marketing for brick-and-mortar businesses). Listing them here keeps the inventory honest — we have the file, we know it exists, we looked and passed — while removing them from the lint orphan-flag without forcing them into a wrong-fit hub.

This page is **librarian bookkeeping**, not a research index. Mirrors the same pattern in `@osint-wiki/concepts/corpus-overflow-out-of-scope.md`. If a source here is later found to belong in another wiki, the correct action is to relocate the raw file and remove the stub — not to keep it indefinitely.

## Narrative

### Inclusion criteria

A source lands here if it was dropped into the ingest queue, a stub was created for inventory completeness, and human triage could not identify any thematic fit with this wiki's clusters. The stub's own `## Narrative` should state why it is out of scope and where it likely belongs.

### Why not just delete?

Deleting the stub loses the record that the file was already triaged-and-rejected, risking duplicate effort on the next sweep. Keeping it under this hub is the "we looked, we passed" marker.

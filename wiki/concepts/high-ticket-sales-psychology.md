## Narrative

### Pattern (from K90 style pass)

@vizionaryfocuss uses **hook-first pain** before solution reveal — typical X Article promo structure for high-ticket services:

1. **Open on buying psychology** — why prospects stall, fear wrong vendor, or undervalue outcomes
2. **Name the pain** before credentials — avoids "agency brochure" tone
3. **CTA: DM/follow** — low-friction next step vs hard sell on first touch

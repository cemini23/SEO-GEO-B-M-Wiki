## Raw Concept

GBP hygiene pointer from Sterling Sky — when Google shows a review as **Edited**, what it means for local trust signals.

## Narrative

When a Google review displays an **Edited** label, it usually means the reviewer changed the text after posting. For local SEO: do not treat edited reviews as fraud by default; monitor for sentiment shifts and document responses. Full post may be paywalled on Sterling Sky — OSINT source holds RSS teaser.

## Narrative

Promotions fill appointment gaps, reactivate dormant clients, and create urgency. Well-timed campaigns aligned to seasonal behavior outperform random discounts by 3-5x.

### Seasonal Campaign Calendar

#### Back-to-School (August-September)

Highest-intent seasonal window. Parents + students resetting routines.

- **"Fresh Start" package**: haircut + beard trim at 20% off regular price
- **Student discount**: 15% off with valid student ID (Tue-Thu)
- **Pep rally special**: $15 buzz cuts for high school athletes
- **Grooming bundle**: haircut + eyebrow threading + mustache trim

> "September marks the true transition into fall — a time of fresh routines. With school back in session, professionals returning to their regular schedules, clients are looking to reset their look." — Zenoti, 2024.

#### Wedding Season (April-June, September-October)

Target grooms, groomsmen, fathers of the bride/groom.

- **Groom's package**: haircut + hot towel shave + beard sculpting 1 week before wedding
- **Groomsmen bundle**: group rate (5+ guys, same day)
- **Father-daughter special**: dad gets 50% off when booking with bride's party
- **Wedding expo tie-in**: partner with local wedding venues for referral discounts

#### Holiday Season (November-December)

Gift-giving season + year-end self-care.

- **Gift cards**: buy $100 card, get $20 bonus credit
- **"New Year, New Look"**: January booking incentive (sell in December)
- **Holiday hours promo**: extended hours in December, early-bird 20% off
- **Stocking stuffer**: beard care kits, premium pomades at 15% off

#### Summer Prep (May-June)

People want to look good for vacations, events, graduations.

- **"Graduation Glow Up"**: senior photos special — haircut + style for $25
- **Vacation ready**: skin fade + lineup + beard sculpt special
- **Summer frizz control**: add-on scalp treatment + moisturizing package

### Weekly Recurring Promotions

| Day | Promotion | Goal |
|-----|-------------|------|
| Monday | "Manic Monday" — $5 off all cuts | Fill slowest day |
| Tuesday-Thursday AM | "Early Bird" — 20% off before 10am + free coffee | Fill morning slots |
| Wednesday | "Ladies Day" — beard trims for women 50% off | Expand demo |
| Friday | "Fresh for the Weekend" — premium cut + hot towel $35 | Capture weekend prep |
| First Tuesday | "Senior Day" — 50% off for 65+ | Community goodwill |

### Tactical Campaign Types

#### Early Bird Specials
Fill morning dead time. $5-7/day ad spend generates $400+/week in otherwise-empty slots [Source: Brandignity 2025].

**Structure**: 20% off morning appointments (before 11am) + free coffee. Promote via Instagram Stories + Google Business Post.

#### Referral Bonuses
Double-sided rewards work best: referrer gets $10 credit, new client gets 20% off.

**Launch method**: 
1. In-shop signage at checkout counter
2. Instagram Story with "Refer a friend" CTA
3. SMS blast to clients who haven't referred yet

#### Flash Sales
24-48 hour urgency. "First 10 bookings this Tuesday get $10 off."

**Best for**: filling unexpected gaps, rainy days, post-holiday lull.

#### Birthday Campaigns
Automated birthday month reward: free upgrade or 50% off.

**Conversion rate**: 10x higher than standard campaigns [Source: FaveCard 2026].

#### Upsell/Cross-sell
Add-on services at checkout:
- Hot towel shave (+$10)
- Beard conditioning treatment (+$8)
- Scalp moisturizing (+$6)

**Key**: offer while client is already in chair, not as separate trip.

### Cross-Promotions with Local Businesses

| Partner | Campaign Idea | Shared Benefit |
|---------|----------------|------------------|
| Coffee shop | "Show your receipt for 15% off" | They drive traffic, you get new clients |
| Gym/Fitness center | "Fresh cut after your workout" | Shared health/wellness audience |
| Men's clothing store | "Suit shopping? Fresh cut 20% off" | Style-conscious demographic |
| Wedding venues | Referral partnership | High-value groom leads |

### Promotion Amplification

| Channel | Best For | Cost |
|----------|----------|------|
| Google Business Profile post | Local reach, free | Free |
| Instagram Stories/Reels | Visual promos, young audience | Free-20/day |
| Google Ads | High-intent "near me" traffic | $5-30/day |
| Meta Ads (FB/IG) | Visual campaigns, retargeting | $5-20/day |
| SMS blast | Existing clients, win-back | $0.01-0.02 per msg |
| In-shop signage | Walk-ins, checkout upsells | One-time print cost |

### Metrics to Track

- **Redemption rate**: % of people who saw promo and used it (target: 5-15%)
- **New vs returning**: did promo attract new clients or just discount existing ones?
- **Revenue per slot**: did promo increase overall revenue or just shift it?
- **Lifetime value of promo clients**: do discount clients become regulars?

**Rule**: if a promo fills slots but attracts no regulars after 3 visits, kill it.

## Raw Concept

Hub page for creator marketing — the discipline of growing, monetizing, and retaining an audience for subscription content platforms (OnlyFans, Patreon, Buy Me a Coffee). Uses a friend's OnlyFans creator journey as the running example. Sources will be ingested progressively; until then, this page frames the shape of the playbook with `[NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-05-08]` tags.

## Narrative

Creator marketing is distinct from traditional business marketing because:

1. **The product is the creator** — trust, parasocial connection, and perceived authenticity drive purchases
2. **Platform-native discovery is weak** — OnlyFans has almost no algorithmic discovery; growth comes from external platforms (Twitter/X, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram)
3. **NSFW constraints** — mainstream platforms restrict or shadowban direct NSFW promotion, requiring careful SFW teaser strategies
4. **Subscription economics** — monthly recurring revenue means retention (churn reduction) is as important as acquisition

### The creator marketing funnel

```
External platforms (Twitter/X, Reddit, TikTok, Instagram)
        ↓
    Profile / Bio link
        ↓
    Free follower / viewer
        ↓
    Paid subscriber (monthly or PPV)
        ↓
    VIP / high-value customer (custom content, tips, tiered subs)
```

Each stage has distinct tactics:
- **Awareness**: external promotion (see @concepts/creator-external-promotion.md)
- **Conversion**: turning free followers to paid (see @concepts/creator-audience-growth.md)
- **Retention**: keeping subscribers from canceling (see @concepts/creator-retention.md)
- **Content**: what to post where (see @concepts/creator-content-strategy.md)

### Content pillars for image-based creators

1. **Teasers** — SFW-leaning images that hint at the full content available on OnlyFans
2. **Behind-the-scenes** — personality content that builds parasocial connection
3. **Engagement posts** — polls, questions, "AMA" style content that drives replies
4. **Value-driven** — tips for followers, "how I got started," industry insights
5. **PPV exclusives** — premium content offered via DM for one-time fee

### First 90 days for a new creator

Detailed 3-phase launch timeline aligned with @briefs/2026-05-08_onlyfans-launch-strategy.md:

- **Phase 1: Pre-launch (days 1–7)**:
  - Set up OnlyFans (verification takes 24–48h; configure tiers per @entities/platforms/onlyfans.md)
  - Create Twitter/X account (SFW content only, bio link to OnlyFans via link-in-bio tool)
  - Build 30+ posts on OnlyFans BEFORE going live (20 SFW teasers + 10 NSFW main content per @concepts/creator-content-strategy.md)
  - Use Claude to brainstorm content angles, draft tweet variations, plan posting schedule

- **Phase 2: Soft launch (days 8–30)**:
  - Go live on OnlyFans; announce on Twitter/X per @concepts/creator-external-promotion.md
  - 3–5 tweets/day on Twitter/X (SFW teasers, "link in bio" CTA)
  - Engage with followers + similar creators (reply to comments, build parasocial connections)
  - Use Claude to analyze which tweets drove most profile visits, refine content strategy
  - Goal: 20–30 subscribers by day 30

- **Phase 3: Growth (days 31–90)**:
  - Add Reddit promotion (after building karma; follow 10:1 rule per @concepts/creator-external-promotion.md)
  - Introduce PPV for premium content ($10–30 per custom set per @entities/platforms/onlyfans.md)
  - Track metrics: subscriber growth (aim +10–20%/month), churn (<15%/month per @concepts/creator-retention.md), revenue
  - Use Claude to analyze metrics, suggest content pivots, draft Reddit posts
  - Goal: 100 subscribers, $1500/month by day 90

### Email Marketing `[CONFIRMED — sources/creator-email-marketing-2026.md]`

Email is the **only audience a creator truly owns**. Unlike social platforms, an email list survives bans, algorithm changes, and shadowbans.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Email ROI | ~$36 per $1 spent |
| Creator email open rates | 20–25% |
| Comment-to-email conversion (Instagram) | 40–60% |
| Lead magnet capture rate | 40–70% |

**Platform selection** — Not all ESPs support adult content:

| Platform | Sex-Work Friendly | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| SendX | ✅ Yes | General creator use |
| YNOT Mail | ✅ Yes | Adult creators |
| EmailOctopus | ✅ Yes | Budget option |
| Systeme.io | ✅ Yes | All-in-one funnels + email |
| Mailchimp | ❌ No | Bans adult content |
| Klaviyo | ❌ No | Bans adult content |

**Creator Flywheel**: `Social Media → Link-in-Bio → Email Capture → Platform Subscription`

Top email strategies: comment-to-DM automation with email gates, welcome sequences, drip campaigns, conversion pushes after 5+ email opens.

### Paid Advertising Readiness `[CONFIRMED — sources/paid-advertising-creators-2026.md]`

Don't start paid ads until organic funnels are proven. Key readiness benchmarks:
- Subscriber acquisition cost < $5/subscriber
- Email capture working to retain paid-ad traffic
- Conversion funnel tested end-to-end

**Multi-platform stat**: Creators active on 3+ platforms earn **$65K/year median** vs. $38K single-platform (ConvertKit 2026).

## Narrative

The signal is Rubio's 7 October 2026 speech at the Pnyx in Athens. He named drones, weapons, warfare, chips, and minerals from the stage. The quote tweet by @crypto_condom is the popular framing.

The thesis rests on three legs. First, policy: the FY2027 defense request is $1.45 trillion, and the missile and munitions pool is about $95 billion, of which CRS attributes about $52.9 billion to the named programs. Second, demand: the Patriot interceptor stockpile is down about 65 to 67 percent, and the rebuild runs to the end of the decade. Third, escalation: Iran runs past the November midterms, and a reported European intelligence assessment puts a Russian Article 5 test in a possible 2027 window.

The eight names, all under about $8 billion except L3Harris:

| Ticker | Group | Role |
|--------|-------|------|
| LHX | inputs | Aerojet Rocketdyne interceptor propulsion |
| MRCY | inputs | Defense microelectronics |
| MP | inputs | Rare-earth magnets |
| AVAV | drones | Switchblade, largest pure play |
| KTOS | drones | Valkyrie, CCA, takeout candidate |
| RCAT | drones | Black Widow, Army SRR |
| ONDS | drones | Counter-UAS roll-up |

Trade structure: hold the five larger names for years, lever with January 2028 or January 2029 LEAPS. Treat RCAT, ONDS, and UMAC as options, not core.

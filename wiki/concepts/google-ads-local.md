## Narrative

Google Ads puts your barbershop in front of locals actively searching for grooming services. Unlike organic SEO, results are immediate — ideal for filling slow periods and capturing high-intent "barber near me" traffic.

### Campaign Types

**Search Campaigns** — text ads on Google SERPs. Target high-intent keywords:
- "barber near me", "haircut [city]", "fade haircut [neighborhood]"
- "men's beard trim", "pompadour haircut [city]"
- Bid on exact + phrase match; use negative keywords to exclude "jobs", "courses", "supply"

**Local Services Ads (LSA)** — pay-per-lead, not per-click. Google screens and badges you. Shows above regular ads with "Google Guaranteed" badge. Best for shops with strong review profiles (4+ stars).

**Display Campaigns** — visual banner ads on partner sites. Use for brand awareness, not direct conversions. Show shop interiors, transformations, team photos.

### Targeting Strategy

- **Radius targeting**: 3-10 miles from shop location. Industry data shows 5 miles is the sweet spot for most barbershops.
- **Dayparting**: concentrate budget on Tue-Fri 10am-6pm (peak booking windows).
- **Device bid adjustments**: mobile +20% (most "near me" searches are mobile).
- **Audience segments**: past website visitors (remarketing), similar audiences, "frequent salon/grooming visitors".

### Budget Guidelines

| Spend Level | Daily Budget | Expected Results |
|-------------|--------------|------------------|
| Test | $5-10/day | 1-3 new clients/week |
| Growth | $15-30/day | 5-10 new clients/week |
| Aggressive | $50+/day | 15+ new clients/week |

Allocate 5-10% of monthly gross revenue to marketing; Google Ads should be ~40% of that budget.

### Landing Page Requirements

**Never send ad traffic to your homepage.** Create dedicated landing pages:
- Match ad copy (e.g., "Skin Fade $25" ad → skin fade service page with clear "Book Now" CTA)
- Mobile-optimized (80%+ bookings happen on mobile)
- Load time <3 seconds (affects Quality Score)
- Include click-to-call button, booking link, and consistent NAP

### Quality Score Factors

- **Expected CTR**: write compelling ad copy with clear value props ("Same-day appointments", "15 years experience")
- **Ad relevance**: tight keyword-to-ad-to-landing-page alignment
- **Landing page experience**: page speed, mobile-friendliness, transparent business info

### Key Metrics to Track

- **Cost per acquisition (CPA)**: target under $15 for new client
- **Conversion rate**: 5-10% is industry average for service landing pages
- **Search Impression Share**: % of eligible searches where your ad appeared
- **Click-through rate (CTR)**: 5-10% for exact match keywords

### Common Mistakes

- Sending ad traffic to homepage instead of service-specific landing pages
- Targeting too broad (city-level when neighborhood-level would convert better)
- Ignoring negative keywords (wasting budget on "barber school" or "barber jobs")
- No call tracking — can't tell which keywords drive phone bookings
- Letting ads run 24/7 when shop is closed

### Integration with GBP

A complete Google Business Profile boosts ad performance:
- **70% more likely to attract visits** when GBP is complete + verified
- **Extensions**: add location extension (pulls from GBP), call extension, booking link
- **Reviews**: higher star rating = higher CTR at same position

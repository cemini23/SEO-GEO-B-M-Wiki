## Raw Concept

Concept page for external promotion — driving traffic from mainstream platforms (Twitter/X, Reddit, Instagram, TikTok) to OnlyFans. The critical growth lever because OnlyFans has almost no native discovery.

## Narrative

**External promotion is 80% of creator growth.** OnlyFans has negligible algorithmic discovery — almost all new subscribers come from clicking a link on an external platform.

### Platform priority for new creators

| Platform | Traffic quality | Ban risk | Difficulty | Priority |
|----------|----------------|----------|------------|----------|
| **Twitter/X** | High (conversion-ready) | Medium (NSFW policy) | Low | **Essential — start here** |
| **Reddit** | Very high (niche-intent) | High (anti-spam) | Medium | **High — after Twitter basics** |
| **Instagram** | Medium (visual discovery) | Low (SFW only) | Medium | Medium |
| **TikTok** | Medium (younger demos) | Low (SFW only) | High (algorithm) | Medium |

### Twitter/X promotion strategy

Twitter/X is the most creator-friendly platform for promotion:

| Tactic | Details | Frequency |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **SFW teaser tweets** | Image + caption hinting at full content on OF | 3–10/day |
| **Engagement tweets** | Reply to followers, big accounts in your niche | Throughout the day |
| **Threads** | Multi-tweet stories ("how I started," "my routine") | 2–3/week |
| **Bio optimization** | Clear value prop + link to OnlyFans/Linktree | Update monthly |
| **Polls & interactive** | "which set should I post next?" | 1–2/week |

**Critical rule**: Never put the direct OnlyFans URL in tweets — use "link in bio" text + bio link. Twitter's algorithm deprioritizes tweets with external links.

### Reddit promotion strategy

Reddit offers the highest-converting traffic but carries the highest ban risk:

| Phase | Action | Timeline |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Karma building** | Comment thoughtfully on niche subreddits; no self-promo | Weeks 1–4 |
| **First posts** | SFW content to relevant subreddits; read each sub's rules carefully | Weeks 4–8 |
| **Bio link introduction** | Add OnlyFans link to bio; mention in context | Weeks 8–12 |
| **Sustained presence** | Regular participation + occasional promo posts (follow 10:1 rule) | Ongoing |

**The 10:1 rule**: For every 1 self-promotional post, make 10 genuine community contributions (comments, answers, discussions). Reddit detects and bans accounts that are "too promotional."

### Instagram promotion strategy

Instagram is fully SFW — use it for brand building, not direct NSFW promotion:

| Tactic | Details | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Portfolio posts** | High-quality SFW images showing your "brand" | Grid = visual resume |
| **Reels** | Short video content (entertaining, behind-the-scenes) | Best reach driver in 2026 |
| **Stories** | Daily behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&As | Ephemeral; builds connection |
| **Bio link** | Linktree/Beacons with OF link + other platforms | Critical element |

**No NSFW on Instagram** — not even "sensitive" flags like Twitter. Fully SFW or risk permanent ban.

### TikTok promotion strategy

TikTok is pure SFW entertainment — viral reach possible but no direct NSFW promotion:

| Tactic | Details | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Trending sounds** | Use trending audio on Reels-like videos | Algorithm boost |
| **Behind-the-scenes** | "Day in the life," workspace tours, getting-ready videos | High entertainment value |
| **Educational content** | "How I set up lighting," "my camera gear" | Longer-tail discovery |
| **Bio link** | Linktree with OF link | Only place to funnel traffic |

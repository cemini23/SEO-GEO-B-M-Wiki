## Raw Concept

Concept page for audience growth — getting new subscribers and converting free followers to paid. The critical discipline for new creators: external traffic → followers → paid subscribers.

## Narrative

Audience growth for subscription creators has two distinct phases: **acquiring free followers** on external platforms, and **converting them to paid subscribers** on OnlyFans.

### Phase 1: Acquiring free followers

| Platform | Tactic | Expected conversion | Source |
|----------|--------|---------------------|--------|
| **Twitter/X** | 3–10 SFW teaser tweets/day + engagement | 1–3% of profile views → followers `[CONFIRMED]` | twitter-x-creator-guide-2026.md |
| **Reddit** | Niche subreddit participation + occasional promo posts | 2–5% of post viewers `[CONFIRMED]` | sources/reddit-creator-promotion-2026.md |
| **Instagram** | Reels + static portfolio posts (SFW only) | 0.5–2% of reach `[CONFIRMED]` | Industry data |
| **TikTok** | Short video content (SFW, entertaining) | 1.9% avg short-to-subscriber conversion per 10K views `[CONFIRMED]` | sources/youtube-shorts-creator-growth-2026.md |

### Phase 2: Converting followers to paid subscribers

The **bio link** is the linchpin. All external promotion funnels to one place: the link in your bio.

| Tactic | How it works | Effectiveness |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Teaser → paywall** | Post SFW image with "full set on my OF (link in bio)" | Core conversion driver; 1–3% of followers |
| **Limited-time offer** | "50% off first month — link in bio" | Lifts conversion 2–5x during promo |
| **Free trial links** | 7–30 day free trials via special link | High conversion; risk of free-tier abusers |
| **PPV samples** | Send a discounted PPV to free followers via DM | Works for warm leads; low volume |
| **Tier pricing psychology** | Low entry tier ($9.99) → upsell to higher tiers | Reduces friction; 30–50% upsell rate |

### The follower-to-subscriber ratio

| Creator stage | Typical ratio |
|---------------|----------------|
| **New (<3 months)** | 50:1 to 100:1 (followers:subscribers) |
| **Established (3–12 months)** | 20:1 to 50:1 |
| **Top creators (12+ months)** | 10:1 to 20:1 |

### Content types that drive subscriptions

Ranked by conversion power for image-based creators:

1. **Before/after or transformation posts** — show the "full version available" hook
2. **Behind-the-scenes glimpses** — "this is what subscribers get every day"
3. **Limited-offer posts** — "24 hours only, 50% off, link in bio"
4. **Q&A / AMA content** — builds trust that drives paid conversion
5. **Preview carousels** — 3–5 SFW images with "full set of 50+ on OF"

### What does NOT drive subscriptions

- **Overly explicit SFW content** — can actually reduce conversion (gives away too much for free)
- **Inconsistent posting** — followers forget you exist; conversion window closes
- **All-promo-all-the-time** — followers mute or unfollow; mix in value/entertainment
- **Buying followers** — zero conversion; ruins engagement ratio; gets accounts banned

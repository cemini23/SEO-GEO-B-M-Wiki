## Raw Concept

Routed from OSINT K57 (`briefs/2026-05-22_k57-bowtied-bull-leadgen-from-osint.md`). Captures a **high-ticket local-service** lead-gen ops model (HVAC, remodeling, cosmetic surgery) that bundles AI qualification + follow-up automation with **review velocity** as the local-search moat. Distinct from the barbershop operator playbook (lower ticket, walk-in heavy) but shares review-acquisition and GEO landing-page tactics.

## Narrative

### Who this is for

- **Agency / solopreneur** selling B2B services to SMBs with **high average job value** ($500+ per job; often $2k–$20k+)
- **Not** the primary frame for the wiki's barbershop running example — barbershops are lower-ticket and relationship-driven; adapt only the review + follow-up layers, not the full performance-pricing agency model

### Offer stack (four layers)

| Layer | What it does | SEO/GEO tie-in |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| **1. Lead filter** | Score inbound leads by urgency + ticket size before human handoff | Reduces wasted ad spend; pairs with @concepts/meta-ads-local.md Lead Gen campaigns |
| **2. Missed-call recovery** | Automated SMS/email when calls go unanswered | Operational retention; indirect — faster response improves conversion, not a ranking factor |
| **3. Dead-pipeline reactivation** | Email/SMS to stale quotes and no-shows | Same; win-back economics |
| **4. Review generation** | Systematic post-job review asks + owner responses | Direct GBP/local-pack signal; see @concepts/reviews-reputation-management.md — **must not** gate or incentivize reviews |

Pricing arc described in source: **performance-based** (per lead or per booked job) → **recurring retainer** once results are proven. [TENTATIVE] — single newsletter; verify against operator counsel before contracting.

### SEO/GEO hooks from the playbook

1. **Star-rating filter on local SERP** — when prospecting SMB clients, prioritize businesses already strong on service quality but weak on review *count* or response discipline; the agency sells the gap between reputation reality and SERP presentation.
2. **Quiz / chatbot intake → optimized landing** — structured Q&A pages that feed both human qualification and @concepts/generative-engine-optimization.md citability (clear entities, FAQs, service-area answers).
3. **Zip-code outbound GTM** — 12-month grind targeting one geography at a time; aligns with @concepts/citation-building.md and local pack concentration rather than national spray.

### Overlap with wiki tools

- **Review replies** — @entities/tools/easy-review.md covers layer 4's *response* side; acquisition still needs compliant in-person + post-service asks per @concepts/reviews-reputation-management.md
- **Claude drafts** — @concepts/review-response-templates.md + marketingskills for SMS/email copy; human approves every send

### Barbershop adaptation (light touch)

Borrow **missed-call SMS** and **post-visit review ask cadence** only. Skip performance-pricing agency packaging unless the operator is explicitly building an agency business.

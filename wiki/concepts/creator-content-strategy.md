## Raw Concept

Concept page for content strategy — what to post, how often, PPV vs subscription-only mix. Covers both OnlyFans wall content and external platform teasers.

## Narrative

Content strategy for subscription creators involves **two parallel streams**:

1. **OnlyFans wall content** — what paid subscribers see (mix of SFW/NSFW depending on creator's brand)
2. **External platform teasers** — SFW content on Twitter/X, Reddit, Instagram, TikTok that drives traffic to OnlyFans

### OnlyFans content mix

| Content type | % of posts | Purpose | NSFW level |
|--------------|-------------|---------|-------------|
| **SFW teasers** | 20–30% | Hook for external promotion; safe to share widely | Fully SFW |
| **NSFW main content** | 40–50% | Core subscriber value; what they pay for | NSFW |
| **Behind-the-scenes** | 10–15% | Parasocial connection; personality building | SFW |
| **PPV exclusive** | 5–10% | High-value one-time purchases via DM | Premium NSFW |
| **Engagement posts** | 5–10% | Polls, questions, "AMA" style | Mixed |

### Posting cadence (OnlyFans wall)

- **New creators**: 1–2 posts/day minimum. Subscribers expect daily content for their $10–$30/month.
- **Established creators**: 1 post/day can work if quality is high.
- **Top creators**: Quality > quantity. 5–7 high-quality posts/week can sustain if engagement is strong.

**Minimum bar**: If subscribers see the same content for 3+ days, churn increases significantly.

### PPV vs subscription-only content

| Approach | Pros | Cons | Best for |
|-----------|------|------|----------|
| **All subscription** | Simple; subscribers get everything | Lower ARPU (avg revenue per user) | New creators building base |
| **Subscription + PPV** | Higher ARPU; premium content monetized twice | More DM management; some subscribers annoyed | Established creators (3+ months) |
| **Mostly PPV** | High per-transaction revenue | High churn; subscribers feel "nickel-and-dimed" | Top creators with dedicated fan base |

**Recommended for new creators**: Start with **all-subscription** to build base, introduce PPV after 90 days for premium/exclusive content.

### External platform content strategy

Each platform needs a distinct approach:

| Platform | Daily volume | Content type | Link policy |
|----------|---------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Twitter/X** | 3–10 tweets | SFW teasers, personality, engagement | Bio link to OF; sensitive media flag for NSFW |
| **Reddit** | 1–3 posts (across subreddits) | Niche-targeted SFW content | Bio link only; no direct OF links in posts |
| **Instagram** | 3–5 posts/week, 2+ Reels | Fully SFW portfolio; lifestyle content | Bio link only; no NSFW allowed |
| **TikTok** | 1 video/day | SFW entertainment; trending sounds | Bio link only; no NSFW |

### Content that converts (proven patterns)

For image-based creators:

1. **The "preview set"** — post 3 SFW images externally, promise 30+ images in full set on OF
2. **The "daily life" series** — morning routine, workspace tour, "what I'm wearing today" (builds parasocial)
3. **The "Q&A answer" post** — take a common question, answer with images + personality
4. **The "limited drop"** — "24 hours only, 50% off, link in bio" (urgency driver)
5. **The "milestone celebration"** — "100 subscribers! special set inside" (social proof)

### What to avoid

- **Over-posting NSFW externally** — gets accounts suspended; use SFW teasers only
- **Reposting identical content across all platforms** — each platform's audience expects different content
- **Long gaps in posting** — algorithm forgets you; followers lose interest
- **Low-effort content** — blurry photos, poor lighting, no personality = no conversion

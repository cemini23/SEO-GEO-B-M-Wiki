## Raw Concept
Created as part of Task 5 of the OnlyFans creator launch plan to define safe AI usage practices for content creators. Synthesized from platform policies, Claude's acceptable use guidelines, and creator workflow best practices.

## Narrative
AI tools like Claude can significantly boost creator productivity, but misuse carries risks: platform bans, AI detection flags, policy violations. These guardrails ensure safe, effective use that keeps the creator's voice intact and accounts secure.

General rules:
- Always personalize AI outputs: never post verbatim
- Avoid generating NSFW content directly (violates Anthropic's usage policies for some contexts)
- No automation scripts for DMs, likes, or follows (guaranteed ban risk on social platforms)
- Use AI for brainstorming, editing, planning — not for full content generation from scratch

## Claude workflow examples

### Content brainstorming
Prompt template: "Give me 10 content ideas for a fitness/lifestyle creator targeting 18-34 year olds, mixing workout tips, meal prep, and behind-the-scenes personal content. Avoid generic ideas, focus on unique angles."

Example output:
1. "What I eat in a day" video breaking down pre/post workout meals with macro counts
2. "Mistakes I made in my first 6 months of fitness content" candid reflection post
3. "Home workout with no equipment" series for followers who can't afford gym memberships
4. "Meal prep fail" relatable post showing imperfect reality of fitness journey
5. "Q&A: Answering your fitness questions" live stream or story takeover
6. "My morning routine" showing consistency habits beyond just workouts
7. "Comparison: Budget vs Premium workout gear" honest review content
8. "Progress photos: 1 year transformation" celebration post with context
9. "Workout playlist reveal" personal touch with music recommendations
10. "Behind the scenes: How I plan my content week" transparency post

### Caption refinement
Before: "Just finished a great workout. Feeling good. #fitness #workout"

After (Claude refined + creator personalized): "2 hours of leg day and I can barely walk 😅 But that post-workout high is unmatched. What's your favorite leg day burner? Drop it below! 👇 #fitnessjourney #legday #personaltraining"

### Metric analysis
What to provide: Last 30 days of subscriber count, post likes, click-throughs to OnlyFans, PPV purchase rate, top 3 performing post types.

What to expect back: Trend lines for growth, identification of top-performing content categories, recommendations for content calendar adjustments (e.g. "increase meal prep content by 20% next week based on 40% higher engagement").

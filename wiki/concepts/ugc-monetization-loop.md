## Raw Concept

Routed from the OSINT workspace — Post 3 of an X-posts batch (`posts.docx`, 2026-05-18) by @timbidefi. Describes a 3-platform UGC monetization loop (TikTok + Instagram + Pinterest) using Claude for pattern extraction and Higgsfield for faceless video generation. Off-topic for OSINT/financial research; primary fit for the creator-marketing vertical.

## Narrative

@timbidefi's thesis: most UGC (user-generated-content) creators are stuck on one platform, one offer, per-video pricing. The "operators" run a **3-platform loop** and charge **retainers**.

### The reverse-engineering loop

On TikTok, filter a target niche by Most-Liked (last 30 days); find 5 videos at 100k+ views that look easy to recreate; hand the links to Claude with a prompt that extracts, per video: the 3-second hook, the psychological lever it pulls, the slide-by-slide structure, why the pattern works for the niche, and 7 hook variations. Pick the 2 strongest patterns as the framework for the next 10 videos — rotate hooks, repeat structure.

### Pinterest as aesthetic library

Prompt Claude for 5 Pinterest search queries that surface the niche's visual language; save 20-30 reference images; feed them to Higgsfield as the visual style guide.

### Production

Higgsfield generates each 30-second faceless vertical UGC video (9:16, 1080×1920) from a templated prompt (setting / presenter / action / tone); product B-roll generated separately and cut in. ~20-30 min per polished video once dialed in.

### Distribution

Same video to TikTok + Instagram within the hour (TikTok captions short/punchy, Instagram slightly longer structured copy). Daily for the first 30 days → 60+ pieces of content as the "audition" for brands.

### The Pinterest angle

Pinterest Idea Pins are an under-served UGC ad surface — lower CPM, older/higher-income buyers, and brands advertising there are starved for native creators. Pitching brands a **3-platform retainer** (TikTok + Instagram + Pinterest) instead of per-video TikTok work moves the creator out of the commodity pool.

### Tooling stack

- **Claude** — brain layer: pattern extraction, scripts, outreach
- **Higgsfield** — faceless video generation
- **TikTok** — primary distribution
- **Instagram** — deal-closing
- **Pinterest** — the differentiator

### Pricing claims

[TENTATIVE — single-source, self-reported]:

- Monthly retainer: $3,000–8,000 for 10–15 videos across 3 platforms
- "$17,000/month realistic within 6 months for one operator"
- Outbound: 15–20 personalized cold emails/week, claimed 8–15% reply rate

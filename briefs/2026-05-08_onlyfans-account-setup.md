## Target
hands-on (friend executes with guide)

## Pre-requisites
- Government ID (front + back)
- Selfie photo (holding ID)
- Bank info OR Paxum/Cosmica account
- Email address (dedicated, not shared with other platforms)
- Phone number (for verification SMS)

## Step-by-step setup

1. **Create account** — go to onlyfans.com, sign up with email + password. Use a dedicated email address.

2. **Verify email + phone** — both required before any payout is possible. Check spam folder for verification emails.

3. **Submit ID verification** — upload ID front/back + selfie holding ID. Processing time: 24-48 hours. Ensure photos are clear, well-lit, and all corners of ID are visible.

4. **Set up payout method** — choose Paxum, Cosmica, or direct bank transfer. Note: each has a minimum payout threshold ($20-50 range depending on method). Paxum is most common for new creators.

5. **Create subscription tiers**:
   - **Entry**: $9.99/month — promise 3-5 posts/day, standard access
   - **Mid**: $24.99/month — priority DM responses + some PPV content at discounted rates
   - **VIP**: $49.99/month — custom content requests + 1-on-1 video calls (limited slots)

6. **Write bio** — SFW tone, teaser style, include "link in bio" CTA directing to OnlyFans. Use Claude to brainstorm angles, then personalize heavily so it sounds like you.

7. **Upload profile photo** — SFW, high-quality, recognizable as you. This is your public face on the platform.

8. **Create FIRST 30 POSTS before going live** — mix of:
   - 20 SFW teasers (clothing, lifestyle, behind-the-scenes)
   - 10 NSFW main content (the value subscribers are paying for)
   Having content ready prevents early cancellations.

## Common mistakes
- **Skipping verification** — you cannot get paid without it; complete this first
- **Launching with 0 posts** — subscribers who see an empty feed cancel within hours
- **Pricing too high/low** — $9.99 entry is standard for new creators; you can adjust later
- **Not testing DM functionality** — make sure you can receive and reply before promoting

## AI assistance

**Use Claude to**:
- Brainstorm tier pricing strategies (then decide yourself)
- Draft bio text options (THEN personalize extensively)
- Plan first 30 posts calendar (what to post, when, mix ratios)

**DON'T**:
- Ask AI to write full posts verbatim — AI detection risk + loses your unique voice
- Generate NSFW content — violates AI platform policies
- Let AI replace your creativity — it assists YOUR vision

**Golden rule**: AI assists YOUR creativity. Every AI output gets personalized before it goes live.

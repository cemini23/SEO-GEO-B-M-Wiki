## 1. PLATFORM DECISION — Pick Your Home Base

### The Short Answer

| If you are... | Go with... | Why |
|--------------|-----------|-----|
| **Traditional creator** (real photos, you are the face) | **OnlyFans** | Largest audience (377M users), 80/20 split, proven path |
| **AI/synthetic persona** (AI-generated character) | **Fanvue** | Explicitly AI-friendly, 85% split, Manager Accounts, no deepfake ban risk |
| **Budget-conscious / testing** | **Passes** | Lowest fee (10%), full feature set |
| **SFW/mainstream** | **Patreon** | 10% flat, no NSFW, large mainstream audience |

### Detailed Comparison

| Factor | OnlyFans | Fanvue | Passes | Patreon |
|--------|----------|--------|--------|---------|
| **Platform fee** | 20% | 15-20% (after intro) | 10% | 10% flat |
| **AI content** | Allowed with `#AI` disclosure; must match verified human | Core use case; explicitly embraced | Allowed with disclosure | SFW only; photorealistic AI adult banned |
| **Payout speed** | 5-7 business days | Immediate withdrawal | Standard | Monthly |
| **Discovery** | None native — 100% external traffic | Some internal algorithm; less saturated | Internal browse | Internal discovery |
| **Creator count** | 4.63M (very competitive) | ~325K (growing fast, less crowded) | Small | 200K+ |
| **KYC** | Government ID + selfie + biometric matching | Same, but Manager Account lets you run personas | Biometric liveness check | Standard ID |
| **Deepfake policy** | ❌ Immediate permanent ban | ✅ Allowed with disclosure | ❌ Banned entirely | ❌ Effectively banned |
| **DM monetization** | ✅ Major revenue source (60-70% of income) | ✅ + built-in AI chat agents | ✅ | ❌ |

### ⚠ Critical Compliance Warning

From the [AI Detection & Enforcement 2026](sources/ai-detection-enforcement-2026.md) source:

> *"35,865 accounts were removed in February 2026 alone for policy violations on OnlyFans."*
> *"Deepfakes, face-swaps, and any AI-generated explicit content featuring real individuals = immediate permanent ban."*
> *"Biometric matching introduced in 2026 goes beyond basic ID checks."*

**Translation:** If you are a traditional human creator on OnlyFans, you can use AI tools for editing/captions with disclosure (`#AI` tag). You **cannot** run a synthetic AI persona. If you want a fully AI-generated character, **Fanvue is the only safe primary platform.**

## 2. PRICING STRATEGY — How to Set Your Rates

### Subscription Tiers (based on 2026 data)

| Tier | Price Range | Purpose | Best For |
|------|------------|---------|----------|
| **Entry** | $4.99–$9.99 | High-volume conversion from free followers | New creators building base |
| **Sweet spot** | $9.99–$19.99 | Balance of volume + revenue | Most successful creators |
| **Premium** | $24.99–$49.99 | VIP perks, custom content, 1:1 interaction | Established creators (3+ months) |

**Key finding:** High-ticket subs ($15-$25) with lower volume outperform low-ticket by **40% in retention**.

### PPV Ladder (Pay-Per-View)

| Tier | Price | Content Type |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Low | $5-$10 | Photo sets, teasers, mini clips |
| Mid | $15-$50 | Premium clips, exclusive content |
| High | $50-$200+ | Custom content, personalized requests |

**Revenue split reality:** Subscriptions are your floor income. **60-70% of total revenue** comes from DMs + PPV + tips.

### What to Do Today

- [ ] Pick 3 tier prices (recommended: **$9.99 / $24.99 / $49.99**)
- [ ] Set PPV range ($10-$50 for starters)
- [ ] Write out what each tier includes (be specific — subscribers buy clarity)

## 3. CONTENT STRATEGY — What to Post Where

### The Content Mix (OnlyFans/Fanvue Wall)

| Content Type | % of Posts | NSFW Level |
|-------------|------------|------------|
| SFW teasers | 20-30% | Fully SFW — safe to share externally |
| NSFW main content | 40-50% | Core subscriber value |
| Behind-the-scenes | 10-15% | SFW — builds parasocial connection |
| PPV exclusive | 5-10% | Premium, via DM |
| Engagement posts | 5-10% | Polls, Q&A, AMA |

**Posting cadence:** 1-2 posts/day minimum for new creators. Subscribers expect daily content for their $10-$30/month. Three days without new posts = immediate churn risk.

### External Platform Strategy

| Platform | Daily Volume | Content Type | Link Rule |
|----------|-------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Twitter/X** | 3-10 tweets | SFW teasers, personality, engagement | Bio link to OF; "link in bio" in every tweet |
| **Instagram** | 3-5 posts + 2+ Reels/week | Fully SFW, visual portfolio | Bio link only |
| **Reddit** | 1-3 posts across subreddits | Niche SFW content | Bio link only; follow 10:1 rule (10 genuine contributions per promo post) |
| **TikTok** | 1 video/day | SFW entertainment, trending sounds | Bio link only |

**The golden rule:** Never put your direct OF link in social posts. Always "link in bio." Twitter's algorithm suppresses external links, and Reddit bans accounts that self-promote too early.

### 5 Content Types That Convert

1. **Preview set** — "3 SFW teasers → full 50-image set on OF"
2. **Daily life series** — morning routine, workspace tours, "what I'm wearing today"
3. **Q&A posts** — answer common questions with images + personality
4. **Limited drops** — "24 hours only, 50% off" (urgency driver)
5. **Milestone celebrations** — "100 subscribers! Special set unlocked"

### What NOT to Do

- ❌ Post NSFW on Instagram/TikTok/Reddit (permanent ban)
- ❌ Cross-post identical content to every platform (looks lazy)
- ❌ Long posting gaps (algorithm forgets you)
- ❌ Start paid ads before organic funnel is proven

## 4. AI ASSISTANCE — What Claude Can & Can't Do

### ✅ Safe Uses

1. **Brainstorm content angles** — give Claude your niche + audience info
2. **Caption variations** — draft your own caption, ask for 5-10 variations
3. **Posting schedule planning** — weekly themes, content mix
4. **PPV pricing analysis** — share your numbers, get optimization suggestions
5. **Research** — platform policy updates, competitor analysis, trending topics
6. **Edit/refine your writing** — clarity, grammar, tone adjustments

### ❌ Never Do These

1. ❌ Generate NSFW images with AI (OF deepfake ban — immediate permanent ban)
2. ❌ Auto-post from AI (violates platform ToS on all platforms)
3. ❌ Automate DMs (kills parasocial connection + ban risk)
4. ❌ Copy-paste AI text without rewriting (AI detectors flag this)
5. ❌ Generate fake engagement or buy followers

### The Right Workflow

```
1. YOU draft rough ideas/bullet points
2. CLAUDE expands, suggests variations, refines
3. YOU rewrite in your own voice, add personal details
4. YOU final edit for tone + add CTAs
5. YOU post with your authentic voice
```

**Critical rule:** Rewrite at least 30-50% of anything AI-generated. Add your slang, humor, personal details. Your audience follows *you*, not a chatbot.

## 5. CONVERSION & RETENTION — The Money Math

### Funnel Benchmarks (2026)

| Stage | Metric |
|-------|--------|
| External → Follower | 1-3% of profile views (Twitter/X), 2-5% (Reddit) |
| Follower → Subscriber | 50:1 to 100:1 ratio (new creator) → improves to 10:1 over time |
| Subscriber → VIP tier | 30-50% upsell rate when offered properly |
| Optimized funnel conversion | 8-15% (vs. <2% average) |

### Churn Prevention

| Subscriber Age | Churn Risk | Strategy |
|---------------|------------|----------|
| Days 1-7 | Up to 25% | Welcome flow, immediate value delivery |
| Week 2-4 | 15-20% | Onboarding DMs, preference questions |
| Month 1-3 | 10-15% | Regular engagement, content variety |
| Month 3-6 | 5-10% | VIP tiers, loyalty rewards |
| Month 6+ | 3-5% | Parasocial bond, personalized content |

**Proven retention levers:**
1. **Reply to DMs in <1 hour** → 30% higher retention
2. **Consistent posting** — never 3+ days without new content
3. **Personalized touches** — remember names, preferences, past conversations
4. **VIP tier upgrades** — higher-tier subs churn at half the rate
5. **Never discount more than 50%** — attracts deal-hunters who churn after the deal ends

### Realistic 90-Day Timeline

| Period | Subscribers | Monthly Revenue | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|-------|
| Days 1-30 | 20-30 | $200-$300 | Soft launch; building base |
| Days 31-60 | 60-80 | $800-$1,000 | Reddit + PPV added |

**Important context:**
- Top 1% of creators earn ~$18,700/month (average)

- Multi-platform creators earn **$65K/year median** vs. $38K single-platform
- 73% of creators earn under $30K/year

Break-even: Month 4-9 for successful builds

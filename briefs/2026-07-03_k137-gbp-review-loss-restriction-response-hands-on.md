## Target

**hands-on** - protect each GBP listing if reviews disappear, new reviews stop publishing, or Google applies a review/rating restriction.

## Summary

July 2026 reports show two separate situations: a possible Google-side missing-review issue, and additive restrictions for repeated fake/incentivized-review violations. Treat them differently: document and escalate bugs; stop campaigns and audit vendors for policy notices.

## Body

### Step 1 - Weekly review snapshot

For each location, record every Friday:

| Location | Review count | Rating | New reviews this week | Screenshot path |
|----------|--------------|--------|-----------------------|-----------------|
| | | | | |

Keep screenshots of GBP public listing + owner dashboard. This gives evidence if counts drop.

### Step 2 - If reviews vanish

1. Screenshot current count/rating and previous baseline.
2. Note any recent fake-review attack or spam-reporting event.
3. Search GBP Help Community for active missing-review incidents.
4. Open/append to a Google support/forum case with:
   - business name/location
   - previous count and current count
   - approximate date/time drop started
   - screenshots
   - whether new reviews are blocked
5. Do **not** start aggressive new review pushes until the issue is understood.

### Step 3 - If Google sends a restriction notice

Restriction wording mentioning "incentivised reviews", fake engagement, or suspicious reviews is policy-driven until proven otherwise.

- [ ] Stop all automated review requests.
- [ ] Run `briefs/2026-07-01_k135-review-compliance-vendor-audit-hands-on.md`.
- [ ] Confirm no discount/reward is tied to reviews, edits, or negative-review removal.
- [ ] Confirm every request goes to customers equally (no gating).
- [ ] Export vendor campaign history before changing settings.
- [ ] Appeal only after the process is fixed and documented.

Repeated violations may stack from 30 days to longer windows. Do not "test" the system.

### Step 4 - Customer communication

If regulars mention their reviews vanished:

> Google is investigating missing reviews on some Business Profiles. We appreciate you taking the time to leave feedback. Please do not repost multiple times; we are documenting the issue with Google.

Avoid promising review restoration.

### Step 5 - Recovery check

After support response or restriction expiration:

- [ ] Confirm old reviews restored or document final loss.
- [ ] Confirm new reviews publish again.
- [ ] Resume only compliant asks using Google review link/QR.
- [ ] Update internal vendor notes.

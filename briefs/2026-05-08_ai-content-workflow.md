## Target
hands-on (friend executes with Claude assistance)

## What Claude CAN do (safe)
- Brainstorm content angles: "give me 10 content ideas for a fitness/lifestyle creator"
- Edit/refine YOUR draft: "here's my caption draft, make it more engaging while keeping my voice"
- Plan content calendars: "given I post 2x/day, create a 7-day content calendar template"
- Research pricing: "what are typical PPV prices for [content type]?"
- Analyze metrics: "here are my last 30 days of subscriber/revenue data, what trends do you see?"
- Draft tweet variations: "here are 3 versions of a teaser tweet, which is most compelling?"

## What Claude should NOT do (risky)
- Write full posts from scratch (detection risk if used verbatim)
- Generate NSFW content (policy violation on some platforms)
- Auto-DM scripts or automation code (ban risk)
- Anything that looks like "AI-generated content" if posted unchanged

## The golden rule
AI assists YOUR creativity. Every output gets personalized, edited, infused with your actual experience/voice.

## Workflow example
1. Creator drafts a caption idea (2-3 sentences, personal)
2. Claude refines: "make this more engaging, keep my casual tone"
3. Creator personalizes the output (adds personal detail, changes wording to match voice)
4. Creator posts to Twitter/X or OnlyFans

## Backup: No AI detected
If a platform flags content as AI-generated, appeal with proof of personalization process.

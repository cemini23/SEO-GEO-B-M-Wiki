## Target
hands-on (friend executes)

## Phase 1: Pre-launch (days 1-7)
- Set up OnlyFans (verification takes 24-48h; complete ID check, set subscription tiers per @entities/platforms/onlyfans.md pricing guidance)
- Create Twitter/X account (SFW content only, bio link to OnlyFans via Linktree/Beacons per @concepts/creator-external-promotion.md)
- Build 30+ posts on OnlyFans BEFORE going live (20 SFW teasers + 10 NSFW main content, following @concepts/creator-content-strategy.md mix ratios)
- Use Claude to: brainstorm content angles, draft tweet variations, plan posting schedule
- Content mix: 20 SFW teasers + 10 NSFW main content

## Phase 2: Soft launch (days 8-30)
- Go live on OnlyFans (announce on Twitter/X per @concepts/creator-external-promotion.md Twitter strategy)
- 3-5 tweets/day on Twitter/X (SFW teasers, "link in bio" CTA, no direct OF URLs to avoid shadowban)
- Engage with followers + similar creators (reply to comments, build parasocial connections per @concepts/creator-audience-growth.md)
- Use Claude to: analyze which tweets drove most profile visits, refine content strategy
- Goal: 20-30 subscribers by day 30

## Phase 3: Growth (days 31-90)
- Add Reddit promotion (after building karma — follow 10:1 rule per @concepts/creator-external-promotion.md Reddit strategy)
- Introduce PPV for premium content ($10-30 per custom set, aligned with @entities/platforms/onlyfans.md PPV pricing guidance)
- Track metrics: subscriber growth (aim +10-20%/month), churn (<15%/month per @concepts/creator-retention.md benchmarks), revenue
- Use Claude to: analyze metrics, suggest content pivots, draft Reddit posts
- Goal: 100 subscribers, $1500/month by day 90

## Success metrics
| Period | Subscribers | Monthly Revenue |
|--------|--------------|------------------|
| Days 1-30 | 20-30 | $200-300 |
| Days 31-60 | 60-80 | $800-1000 |
| Days 61-90 | 100+ | $1500+ |

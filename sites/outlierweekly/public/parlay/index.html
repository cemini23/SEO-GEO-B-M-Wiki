The Parlay Is the Price on the Submit Screen

The sportsbook ticket is the displayed American on the submit screen. Multiplying the legs and calling that EV is how a screenshot becomes a bankroll problem.

The letter

The 21 September 2026 issue The Parlay Is the Price on the Submit Screen. is the canonical write-up. CeminiParlays prices Hard Rock parlays from that in-app American. CeminiDFS is the other contest. The public gambling wiki is the memory. Four Grok desks research the slate and recap the ledger. They do not click Bet.

Week 1 graded two hits of five tickets, $40 risked, +$26.07. That is one Sunday. It is not an edge. This page does not reprint the letter and does not publish a next-slate card.

GuruWatcher — Discord only fires when the level is real

Outlier Weekly's GuruWatcher letter is about alert discipline: a newsletter prints a price, a local app checks the market, and Discord pings only when that printed number is hit. No orders. You make the call.

The letter

The July 2026 issue Discord Only Fires When the Newsletter Level Is Real is the public write-up. Soft lists with no numbers do not arm. Chart-only or vague “watch this name” text does not fire. The product reads concrete levels from a newsletter you already have the right to use, looks the market up on a timer, and messages your Discord when a check sees a hit.

What GuruWatcher is

GuruWatcher is a self-hosted Mac and Windows app. You bring the newsletter source and a Discord webhook. It runs on your computer. It is alert-only: it never places orders, it is not a broker, and it does not tell you what to buy. After checkout you download the app from the thank-you page. There is no hosted watch service and no bundled newsletter feed.

Price on the product site is a one-time fee. Outlier Weekly does not sell the app and does not take the payment. Read the product Terms and FAQ before you buy.

What it is not

  • Not a trading bot. No orders. Alert-only.
  • Not investment advice. A Discord ping is not a trade instruction.
  • Not Watcher Guru / watcher.guru. Different product, different site.
  • Not a newsletter reseller. You bring sources you have the right to use. The app does not redistribute paid letter text.

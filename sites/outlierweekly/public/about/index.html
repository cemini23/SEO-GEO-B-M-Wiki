About Outlier Weekly

Who writes the letter, what it covers, and — just as important — what it is not.

Who writes it

Outlier Weekly is written by Cemini23, an operator who builds and runs the tools behind the letter. The same person ships Atto (youratto.com), a paperwork pipeline built around a real family story; runs GuruWatcher (guruwatcher.com), an alert service with paid levels behind it; and open-sources the World Cup LP bot (github.com/cemini23/world-cup-bot) that trades World Cup 2026 prediction markets on Polymarket and Kalshi.

The letter started in May 2026. Every issue applies a three-formula framework to one rare-event market, then records how the operator's own research harness — Cursor, DeepSeek, and Grok running in parallel — produced the position. That mix is deliberate: the market analysis is the letter's spine, and the operator notes are the proof that the framework is used, not just described.

A short history

Outlier Weekly launched in May 2026. As of August 2026 the archive holds 14 letters — roughly 25,000 words — and every one of them is free to read. The letter has no paywall and no sections; it is a flat, dated record of one operator's research. The archive is the full list, and RSS carries every issue.

How the letter is made

Each issue runs the same loop. The operator picks one market, applies the three-formula system from Methodology, and writes up the result. The research itself runs through the agent harness: Cursor as the editor, DeepSeek and Grok in parallel as separate analysis lanes, and a linted LLM wiki as the memory. The market work is the spine; the operator notes are the proof the framework is actually used.

What the letter covers

  • Mispriced rare-event opportunities on Polymarket and Kalshi.
  • The three-formula system behind each position (see Methodology).
  • Operator notes: Atto paperwork, GuruWatcher alert design, wiki linting, and the Cursor/DeepSeek/Grok agent harness.
  • Dossiers on specific markets, including the CXW / GEO / Target Hospitality research in CXW/GEO.

What it is not

  • Not a broker or an adviser. No order execution, no managed accounts, no per-trade recommendations. Letters are research notes written in public.
  • Not a hosted bot service. The World Cup LP bot is open source. You run it yourself; the operator does not operate it for you.
  • Not Outlier AI, Outlier.com, or Outlier.org. Those are unrelated companies. Outlier Weekly is an independent newsletter by an individual operator.
  • Not investment advice. Nothing in the letters or on this site is a solicitation to buy or sell any security or contract.

Where the letters live

Letters publish on Substack and stay there as the canonical copy: the publication's About page, the archive, and the RSS feed. This domain, outlierweekly.com, is the owned hub: it links to the letters and hosts the evergreen pages that describe the letter, its system, and its tools.

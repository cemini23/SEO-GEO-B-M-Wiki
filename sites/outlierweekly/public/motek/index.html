⚠️ 3 Spec-Binder Contradictions to Know

Dish & PageWhat the Binder PrintsHow to Handle It on the Test
Greek Lemon Potatoes (Page 43) Header has GF and V (Vegetarian) badges, but the ingredient list includes Chicken broth. If you are asked for the ingredients, include Chicken broth (with Idaho potatoes, garlic, lemon juice, preserved lemon, hawayej, fresh rosemary). Confirm with your trainer whether the key grades it V or not vegetarian.
Roasted Eggplant (Cold Mezze) (Page 15) Marked GF and VG with 5 vegan ingredients (eggplant, lemon tahini, pine nuts, sumac, mint), yet the allergy line prints Eggs, sesame, nuts. The allergy line was copied from Roasted Eggplant Hummus (p.8), which has a fried poached egg. The cold mezze has no egg in its build, but "Eggs" stays on the p.15 allergy line.
Gelato (Page 57) & Lemon Tahini (Page 51) Page 57 prints Nuts → Mango under Gelato allergies. Page 51 prints Sesame, add pepper under Lemon Tahini allergies. Binder typos. Florida Gelato carries 3 flavors: Madagascar vanilla, Nutella, Mango sorbet. New York adds Dark chocolate, Motek amba, and Black tahini.

World Cup LP Bot

An open-source liquidity-provider bot for World Cup 2026 prediction markets on Polymarket and Kalshi — built, shadow-tested, open-sourced, and documented in Outlier Weekly.

What it is

The World Cup LP bot provides liquidity on World Cup 2026 event markets. Cemini23 built it, ran it in shadow mode first, and then open-sourced it in June 2026. The code, the setup guide, and the tournament results are all public. This page is the owned hub that ties them together.

Shadow-test first

The bot was not thrown into live markets on day one. It ran in shadow mode first: the bot generated simulated fills against live market data without risking capital. Only after the shadow results looked right was the code open-sourced, and only after that was it run for real. The June 2026 letters document each step in order — build, shadow-test, open-source, live run, tournament.

Why a liquidity-provider bot

Event markets only work when someone quotes both sides. A liquidity provider (LP) places two-sided orders and earns the spread between them, accepting inventory risk in exchange. On a fast-moving World Cup market, doing that by hand is slow and inconsistent; a bot can hold both quotes, react to price moves, and keep the book tight. That is the job this bot automates — with the caveat that inventory risk is real, and the tournament letter records exactly how that played out.

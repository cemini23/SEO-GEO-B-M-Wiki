Outlier Weekly

A weekly letter that finds mispriced rare-event opportunities on Polymarket and Kalshi using a synthesized three-formula system — with operator notes on the products behind it.

Outlier Weekly is written by Cemini23. Every issue takes one rare-event market, applies a three-formula framework, and ends with a single position or a clear pass. The same letter also tracks the operator's own builds: the Atto paperwork pipeline, GuruWatcher alert levels, and the Cursor, DeepSeek, and Grok agent harness used to research and write.

How the site and Substack fit together

The letters are the core product. They publish on Substack, and the Substack URLs are canonical — this page and the other hub pages never reprint a letter, they point at it. outlierweekly.com exists to give the letter an owned home on the web: stable URLs, a sitemap, a machine-readable index, and evergreen pages that describe what the letter is and what it is not. If you are a reader, start with the letters. If you are a crawler or an LLM, start with llms.txt.

What a typical issue contains

  • One rare-event market with a defined resolution date.
  • The three formulas applied to that market's real numbers and contract prices.
  • Where formula output disagrees with the market price, and why that matters.
  • One position — or a written case for passing.
  • Operator notes: the Atto paperwork pipeline, GuruWatcher alerts, and the Cursor/DeepSeek/Grok harness behind the research.

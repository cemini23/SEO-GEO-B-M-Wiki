Outlier Weekly

A weekly letter that finds mispriced rare-event opportunities on Polymarket and Kalshi using a synthesized three-formula system — with operator notes on the products behind it.

Outlier Weekly is written by Cemini23. Every issue takes one rare-event market, applies a three-formula framework, and ends with a single position or a clear pass. The same letter also tracks the operator's own products: Atto (Italian civil-record paperwork) and GuruWatcher (Discord alerts when a newsletter price is actually hit).

How the site and Substack fit together

The letters are the core product. They publish on Substack, and the Substack URLs are canonical — this page and the other hub pages never reprint a letter, they point at it. outlierweekly.com exists to give the letter an owned home on the web: stable URLs, a sitemap, a machine-readable index, and evergreen pages that describe what the letter is and what it is not. If you are a reader, start with the letters. If you are a crawler or an LLM, start with llms.txt.

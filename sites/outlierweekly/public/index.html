Outlier Weekly

A weekly letter on investing and AI. One subject per issue — a metal, a model, a market, or a product the operator ships — with the numbers written in the open.

Outlier Weekly is written by Cemini23. Recent letters cover mines, private names, a parlay price, and two products the operator ships: Atto (Italian civil-record paperwork) and GuruWatcher (Discord alerts when a newsletter price is actually hit). The first issues priced rare-event contracts on Polymarket and Kalshi. Methodology says how an issue is built now, and where that first system lives.

How the site and Substack fit together

The letters are the core product. They publish on Substack, and the Substack URLs are canonical — this page and the other hub pages never reprint a letter, they point at it. outlierweekly.com exists to give the letter an owned home on the web: stable URLs, a sitemap, a machine-readable index, and evergreen pages that describe what the letter is and what it is not. If you are a reader, start with the letters. If you are a crawler or an LLM, start with llms.txt.

CXW / GEO / Target Hospitality — research dossier

Outlier Weekly's working file on the US private detention-services complex: CoreCivic (CXW), GEO Group (GEO), and Target Hospitality (TH). Research notes, not a trade recommendation.

The companies

CoreCivic (CXW) and GEO Group (GEO) are the two largest private prison and detention-services operators in the United States. Target Hospitality (TH) provides specialty lodging and related services, including facilities used for migrant detention. The three names appear together in Outlier Weekly's research because their financial structures and their real estate footprints have moved in step.

Why these three names appear together

They are different businesses with different balance sheets. Outlier Weekly tracks them as one complex: the same policy cycle moves their contracts, their real estate, and their market narratives together. A claim about one name in the letters is usually tested against the other two.

The thread, in order

The dossier is a reading order, not a stack of separate posts. The July 3 letter (The $1.1B Warehouse Failure Behind the CXW/GEO Trade) builds the asset-level case. The July 8 letter (CoreCivic Sold Facilities — CXW, GEO, TH) tracks what the facility sales mean for the three names. The August 24 letter (The Short Is Not the Perp. It Is the Listing.) restates the whole thread in one framing. New readers should follow that order.

### Unique angle  
Most models miss that **LLMs cite based on URL stability + semantic clarity**, not just content quality. A 301-redirected domain fails both: the final URL is third-party, and the redirect chain obscures topical authority. Fixing the canonical host isn’t just SEO—it’s foundational for AI discoverability.
